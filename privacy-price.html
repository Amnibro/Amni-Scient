Amni-Price Privacy Policy

Apps: Amni-Price for Android (package com.amniscient.price) and Amni-Price for iPhone and iPad (bundle ID com.amniscient.price). Effective: October 8, 2026 (added the iOS app). Publisher: Amniscient, LLC. Contact: amnibro7@gmail.com.

Amni-Price records the prices you scan so you can compare stores. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics of our own and no tracking or advertising SDKs. We run no server for it: your prices, stores, shopping list and location stay on your phone. Amni-Price is open source under the GPL-3.0 at github.com/Amnibro/Amni-Price, so anyone can check what it does. This policy explains the little that does leave your phone, to whom, and why.

The sections from "What is sent, and where" to "Permissions" describe the Android app. The iPhone and iPad app is described in Amni-Price for iOS. "Children", "Your choices" and "Changes" apply to both.

On your phone only

  • Camera. Shelf tags and receipts are read on the phone as you scan. Images are not saved or uploaded. If you pick a receipt photo from your gallery, the app reads it on the phone and does not copy it.
  • Prices, products, stores, shopping list and settings are stored in the app's private database. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.
  • Location is optional. When you allow it, the app uses it to pin a store where you are, show distances to stores and suggest the store you are standing in. Your position is stored only as saved store locations on your phone and is never sent anywhere. The app does not use location in the background.
  • Sharing and export. "Share prices" and CSV export hand text or a file to the app you choose in Android's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you do it.
  • Backups. If you use Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's data in your backup; we never see it.

Permissions

  • Camera: scan shelf tags, barcodes and receipts.
  • Location (precise and approximate, optional): pin stores, distances and "You're at this store?" suggestions. The app works without it.
  • Internet and network state: map tiles, community price downloads when you ask for them, and the ML Kit diagnostics described above (Google Play build only).

Amni-Price for iOS (iPhone and iPad)

The iOS app does the same job as the Android app with Apple's built-in frameworks. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking or advertising SDKs, and it sends no device or advertising ID. We run no server for it. It does not use Google ML Kit or OpenStreetMap map tiles. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected, and its privacy manifest declares no collected data and no tracking.

On your device only

  • Camera. Shelf tags, barcodes, receipts and menus are read on the device with Apple's VisionKit and Vision frameworks. Images are not saved or uploaded.
  • Photos. If you pick a receipt photo or screenshot, iOS's photo picker hands the app only the picture you chose. The app reads it on the device and does not copy it. The app has no access to the rest of your photo library.
  • Prices, products, stores, shopping list and community prices are stored in a file in the app's private storage, and settings in the app's preferences. Settings › Erase all data, Remove community prices or deleting the app removes them.
  • Location is optional and asked for only when you use a feature that needs it. It is used to pin a store where you are, show distances to stores, suggest the store you are in and pick the community price area. Apart from the geocoding lookup above, your position is stored only as saved store locations on your device. The app does not use location in the background.
  • Sharing and export. "Share prices" and CSV export hand text or a file to the app you choose in the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you do it.
  • Backups. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup; we never see it.

Permissions

  • Camera: scan shelf tags, barcodes, receipts and menus.
  • Location While Using the App (optional): pin stores, distances, "You're at this store?" suggestions and the community price area. The app works without it.
  • No photo library permission is requested; the iOS photo picker is used instead.

Children

Amni-Price is a shopping tool meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke camera or location permission at any time in Android settings, or in the iOS Settings app.
  • Delete your data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. On iOS, use Settings › Erase all data in the app or delete the app.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to look up or delete. Questions: amnibro7@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
