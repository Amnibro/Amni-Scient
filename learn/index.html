ℹ️ About Amni-Learn — what you can practice & age levels

About Amni-Learn

Amni-Learn is a free, ad-supported educational playground built for learners from preschool through adulthood. Eight age-tiered levels let a parent, teacher, tutor, or self-directed learner pick the band that fits — pre-K letter tracing on up to adult cognitive training — and start interactive practice immediately. No signups, no tracking, no data leaves your device. Every game runs entirely in your browser.

Created and curated by Amnibro, a mechanical engineer and independent educator, Amni-Learn is part of the Amni-Scient suite of privacy-first scientific and educational tools. All scores, streaks, and leaderboards are stored locally in your browser using a single localStorage key — nothing is uploaded.

What You Can Practice

  • School Skills: letter and number tracing, early math, matching and memory, keyboard typing, connect-the-dots drawing
  • Life Skills: telling time on an analog clock, counting money and making change, chore and task simulations
  • Science & Nature: biology, chemistry, physics, Earth science quizzes with explanations
  • Reading & Language: phonics, vocabulary, sight words, grammar, short reading passages
  • Music Studio: note recognition, rhythm practice, a free-play instrument layout for composition
  • Brain Training (Adult): Sudoku, card-pair memory, speed math, word search, logic puzzles

Age Bands & Levels

  • Level 1 — Pre-K (ages 3–5): tracing, animal sounds, simple counting, shape matching
  • Level 2 — K–1st (ages 5–7): phonics, single-digit addition & subtraction, sight words
  • Level 3 — 2nd–3rd (ages 7–9): multiplication, broader vocabulary, beginner science
  • Level 4 — 4th–5th (ages 9–11): fractions, geography, intermediate reading comprehension
  • Level 5 — Middle School: pre-algebra, introductory chemistry and biology
  • Level 6 — High School: advanced STEM, algebra, essay-level reading
  • Level 7 — College Prep: logic puzzles, analytical reasoning
  • Level 8 — Adult Brain Health: cognitive exercises for focus, memory, processing speed

How It Works

Every module loads instantly from a single static HTML page — no backend, no account, no cookies beyond the local score key. Pick a level from the URL with ?level=1 through ?level=8, or land on the Amni-Learn product page and use the level selector there.

Progress is saved per-browser. The fullscreen button works on any device, and the game grid re-flows to fit phones, tablets, and desktops. Nothing is sent to a server — the entire app runs from a single static HTML page, so it works fully offline (airplane mode, school WiFi blocking, no signal — all fine). No trackers, no accounts, and after the first load, no network requests at all.

Found a bug or want a new subject? Reach out through the About page.
