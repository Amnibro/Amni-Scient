HOW IT WORKS

1. PICK AN AGE

Choose any age from 1 to 13. Activities and worksheets come from six developmental bands — including a caregiver-guided toddler packet for ages 1–2 with extra-big print throughout.

2. FOUR PAGES, FRESH DAILY

Classic mode: Page 1 is a balanced six-category checklist. Adventure mode: a themed quest path with five stations, side quests, and stamp track. Pages 2–3 rotate age-tuned worksheets (more volume + stretch challenges in Adventure). Page 4 is the week's supplies checklist.

3. GATHER ONCE, PLAY ALL WEEK

The supplies page unions the actual upcoming week of activities into one shopping-style checklist, so everything's on hand before it's needed.

WHY SCREEN-FREE ACTIVITIES

Amni-Learn's games are built for focused digital practice, but paper-and-hands activities build a different set of skills — pencil grip, scissors control, following multi-step instructions away from a screen, and the patience of a slower-paced task. The do-together half leans into practical life: planting seeds, washing vegetables, spreading bread, wiping tables — real skills children are proud of. The independent half builds focus and confidence working alone. For toddlers, every activity is a 5-minute caregiver script, because at ages 1–2 the "worksheet" is really shared play with a grown-up.
