HOW IT WORKS

1. PICK AN AGE

Choose any age from 3 to 13. Activities are grouped into five developmental bands so difficulty and vocabulary match where your child actually is.

2. TODAY'S SIX

One activity from each of six categories is chosen for the day — balanced across reading, math, fine motor, science, creative arts, and life skills.

3. PRINT & CHECK OFF

The sheet fits one to two printed pages with big checkboxes, so a child can mark off each activity as it's finished.

WHY SCREEN-FREE ACTIVITIES

Amni-Learn's games are built for focused digital practice, but paper-and-hands activities build a different set of skills — pencil grip, scissors control, following multi-step instructions away from a screen, and the patience of a slower-paced task. Rotating six categories every day keeps one subject from crowding out the others, and picking the same set all day (instead of re-rolling on every page view) means a sheet printed in the morning still matches whatever's left to do that evening.
