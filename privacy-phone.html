Amni-Phone privacy

Amni-Phone is a design studio that runs in your browser. Published by Amniscient, LLC. Contact: amnibro7@gmail.com.

  • No account and no upload. The page does not send your design to Amni-Scient. There is no analytics and no ad network.
  • Saved designs stay in local storage on that browser, on that machine. Clearing site data deletes them. Export writes a file you choose to keep.
  • Datasheet and store links open only when you click them, and then you are on that other site.
  • The optional board helper is a program you run yourself on 127.0.0.1. The hosted page does not start it and does not reach it unless you do.
