Amni-Drive Privacy Policy

App: Amni-Drive for Android (package com.amni.drive). Effective: November 26, 2026. Publisher: Amniscient, LLC. Contact: privacy@amni-scient.com.

Amni-Drive is a car dashboard and USB Android Auto replacement. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry, and no tracking or advertising SDKs. Everything you configure — themes, cockpit layouts, widget preferences, and web sign-in cookies — stays on your device.

Permissions

  • Location: speedometer, compass, weather widget, and built-in map navigation.
  • USB Accessory: connect directly to your car’s factory Android Auto USB port.
  • Install packages (REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES): install SHA-256-verified OTA updates and companion Amni apps when you tap Install/Update.
  • Notification listener (optional): display Now Playing media controls and incoming message banners on the dash.
  • Display over other apps (optional): open the dashboard automatically when connecting while your phone screen is locked.
  • Microphone (optional): voice destination search when you tap the mic button.

Your choices & removal

Uninstalling Amni-Drive removes all stored preferences, cached tiles, and WebView cookies from your device. Questions? Email privacy@amni-scient.com.
