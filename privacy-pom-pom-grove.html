Privacy Policy

App: Pom-Pom Grove for iPhone and iPad (bundle ID com.amniscient.pomlinggrove). Effective: October 22, 2026. Publisher: Amniscient, LLC. Contact: amnibro7@gmail.com.

Data Not Collected

Pom-Pom Grove does not collect, send or share any information about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking and no advertising or third-party SDKs, and it makes no network connections. We run no server for it.

What stays on your device

  • Your grove. Your Pomlings, items, gardens, progress and up to three save slots are stored in the app's own storage on your device, along with a few automatic backup copies so a damaged save can be recovered.
  • Settings such as volume, haptics, text size and controls are stored the same way.
  • Files app. The app's save folder is visible in the Files app on your device, so you can copy or back up your saves yourself. Nothing is copied anywhere unless you do it.
  • Device backups. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that backup. We never see it.

Permissions

Pom-Pom Grove asks for no permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location, and it does not send notifications from a server.

Children

Pom-Pom Grove is a family game that anyone can play. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your choices

  • Delete a save slot from the profile screen in the game, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete. Questions are welcome at amnibro7@gmail.com.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that sends any data, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date above, and the App Store privacy label will change to match.
