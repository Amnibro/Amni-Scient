Privacy Policy

App: Pom-Pom Grove for iPhone, iPad and Android (bundle ID and package name com.amniscient.pomlinggrove). Effective: October 27, 2026. Publisher: Amniscient, LLC. Contact: amnibro7@gmail.com.

Data Not Collected

Pom-Pom Grove does not collect, send or share any information about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking and no advertising or analytics SDKs, and it makes no network connections. On Android, AR body tracking uses Google’s MediaPipe library, which runs entirely on your device. We run no server for it.

What stays on your device

  • Your grove. Your Pomlings, items, gardens, progress and up to three save slots are stored in the app's own storage on your device, along with a few automatic backup copies so a damaged save can be recovered.
  • Settings such as volume, haptics, text size and controls are stored the same way.
  • Files app (iPhone and iPad). The app's save folder is visible in the Files app on your device, so you can copy or back up your saves yourself. Nothing is copied anywhere unless you do it.
  • Device backups. If you use iCloud Backup, back up your device to a computer, or turn on Android backup to your Google account, your device may include the app's data in that backup. We never see it.

Permissions

Pom-Pom Grove asks for two optional permissions, each only when you use the feature that needs it. Say no and everything else still works.

  • Camera (AR only). When you turn on AR, the front camera shows you on screen and body tracking finds your pose so your Pomling can copy your moves. Each frame is processed on your device as it arrives and is never saved, recorded or sent anywhere. The camera turns off when you leave AR.
  • Motion and fitness or physical activity (Pocket Garden only). When a Pomling rides along on a walk, the game reads your phone's step count to turn steps into stars. Only the step count is read, it stays in your save, and it is never sent anywhere.

It does not use the microphone, photos, contacts or location, and it does not send notifications from a server.

Children

Pom-Pom Grove is a family game that anyone, including children, can play. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. The camera and step features work only on the device, and a parent can decline either permission.

Your choices

  • Delete a save slot from the profile screen in the game, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete. Questions are welcome at amnibro7@gmail.com.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that sends any data, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date above, and the App Store privacy label and the Google Play data safety section will change to match.
