Suggestibility, predicted from the lab dose

Kloft and colleagues vaporized 300 μg of THC per kilogram, about 21 mg at 70 kg. A suggested false detail was accepted 19% of the time after THC (SE 0.04) and 8% after placebo (SE 0.02). Cohen’s d is 0.62.

IQ, predicted from study potency

Adolescent-onset persistent cannabis users in Dunedin lost 8 IQ points from childhood to age 38. DEA-seized flower over 1995–2010 averaged 7.1% THC. That is the study rate on this chart.
