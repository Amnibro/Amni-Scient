Installing an APK

An APK is the same app file the Play Store installs, just downloaded directly. Android asks once before it lets your browser install apps, and that’s normal.

Download

Open the download link on your Android phone. Your browser may warn that the file can harm your device. Tap Download anyway.

Allow the install

Open the file. If Android says your browser isn’t allowed to install apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

Install and play

Tap Install and open it like any other app. You can turn the browser permission back off afterward.

      Questions

      Is it safe to install an APK?

      Yes, as long as it comes from someone you trust. These files come straight from this site, the same builds I test myself. Android’s warning is shown for every APK from outside the Play Store, not because something is wrong with this one. Play Protect still scans it when it installs.

      Do I need a Google account?

      No. Any email works, and the APK installs without the Play Store.

      How do updates work?

      When there’s a new build, I email you a fresh download link. Install it over the old one and your progress stays. Amni-Chat and Amni-Map are signed by Google Play, so once their Play tests open, Play updates them for you.
