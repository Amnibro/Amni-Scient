Braid Privacy Policy

Effective 14 August 2026  ·  Last updated 14 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Amniscient, LLC, a New York limited liability company (“Amniscient”, “we”, “us”), handles information in connection with Braid, a Windows desktop application that orchestrates locally installed AI clients and optional local models.

Braid is not an artificial-intelligence service. Amniscient does not operate the language models you use in Braid and does not interpose a proxy between your computer and those providers.

Related documents: product privacy index · Braid product page · Terms.

1. Roles and scope

Controller. Amniscient is the controller of the limited licence and billing information described in Section 4.

Not a processor of chat content. Prompts, completions, files, workspace contents, and credentials for third-party AI services are processed on your device and, where you have configured a cloud seat, by that third party under its own terms. Amniscient does not receive that content.

Independent controllers. Anthropic, xAI, Google, OpenAI, Stripe, and any other provider you enable are independent controllers of the data you send them. This Policy does not govern their processing.

2. Information processed on your device

The following is stored locally under Braid’s application directory and any working folder you designate. Amniscient has no remote access to it.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Licence administration

To activate a paid or trial entitlement, and approximately twice per day thereafter while the application can reach the network, Braid submits an HTTPS request to Amniscient’s licence service containing:

  • the licence key (or a one-time checkout bootstrap token);
  • a 24-character SHA-256 digest derived from hostname and network interface identifier (the raw identifiers are not transmitted);
  • a truncated hostname (at most 60 characters) used only as a device label in the application.

The service returns a signed entitlement (tier, expiry, key fingerprint, machine binding). If the device is offline, a previously cached entitlement remains valid until its stated expiry. Deactivation instructs the service to release that machine binding.

This channel does not include prompts, transcripts, file paths, or API keys.

3.2 Payments

Subscriptions and prepaid token allocations are processed by Stripe, Inc. Payment-card and wallet data are submitted to Stripe and are not available to Amniscient. Stripe provides Amniscient with the information required to issue or replenish a licence (typically purchaser email, payment status, and amount). See the Stripe Privacy Policy.

3.3 Correspondence

If you contact us (for example at amnibro7@gmail.com), we retain the message in order to respond. Braid does not enrol users in marketing lists.

3.4 Optional tester reports

Users on the tester plan may submit in-application feedback. That facility removes credential material and filesystem paths before a report is assembled. Submission is voluntary.

4. Information sent to third-party AI providers

When you enable a cloud seat, Braid constructs a prompt for that turn (your question and any context you have permitted, which may include other seats’ answers, memory cells, or file excerpts) and delivers it through the client or API you configured. Retention, logging, and any use for model improvement are determined by that provider and by the settings on your account with them.

Local seats (including Ollama and the bundled Adam model) perform inference on the device. No network transmission is required for those seats.

Braid sets process environment variables intended to disable vendor-CLI telemetry where those tools honour them, including DO_NOT_TRACK, DISABLE_TELEMETRY, DISABLE_ERROR_REPORTING, OTEL_SDK_DISABLED, Claude Code non-essential traffic flags, and Gemini usage-statistics flags. An optional hardener can write the documented opt-outs into Claude and Gemini local settings files.

Account-level training or retention controls (for example on claude.ai, Google Account data settings, xAI, or OpenAI) are not overridden by Braid. Users who require those opt-outs must configure them with the relevant provider.

5. Purposes and legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, Amniscient processes licence and payment-related information to perform the contract for the software (issue, validate, and revoke entitlements) and, for correspondence, on the basis of legitimate interests in providing support. We do not process chat content.

6. Sharing

Amniscient does not sell personal information. Licence and payment metadata may be processed by hosting and payment subprocessors solely to operate checkout and entitlement. Chat content is not shared by Amniscient because it is not received.

7. Retention

  • Device. Local stores remain until you delete them or uninstall the application.
  • Licence records. Retained as needed to honour, revoke, or transfer a paid key (key, machine fingerprints, tier, expiry). Upon request after cancellation we will delete the licence record except where we must keep a minimal payment reconciliation record.
  • Stripe. Retained under Stripe’s policy.
  • Provider accounts. Retained under each provider’s policy.

8. Security

Licence entitlements are verified with Ed25519 signatures. The signing private key is held on the licence service and is not shipped in the application. Transport to the licence service and to Stripe uses TLS. Local configuration permissions follow ordinary desktop practice (restricted file mode on non-Windows systems).

9. Children

Braid is intended for professional and adult use. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International users

The licence service and Stripe may process information in the United States and other jurisdictions where those services operate. By using a paid Braid licence you acknowledge that licence-validation data may be transferred to those locations.

11. Your choices

You may delete local data by removing Braid’s configuration directory and workspace. You may request deletion of licence records we hold by writing to amnibro7@gmail.com. Export and deletion of conversations held by a model provider must be requested from that provider.

12. The amni-scient.com website

Some pages on the marketing website may display advertising served by Google AdSense. That processing is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and applies to the website only. The Braid application does not contain advertising software.

13. Changes

We may revise this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Material revisions will be noted on the Braid product page when practicable. Continued use of the software after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy to the extent permitted by law.
