AMNI-LLM — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 2026-04-27  |  Last Updated: 2026-04-27

This privacy policy describes how Amni-LLM, an in-browser GGUF language-model runtime developed by Amni-Scient, handles user information. Amni-LLM is open-source under the MIT License (github.com/Amnibro/amni-llm).

1. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect no personal data. Amni-LLM has no accounts, no login, no telemetry endpoint, and no Amni-Scient-operated server that receives user data. Inference runs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly.

2. DATA HANDLING

Your prompts, model weights, and generated replies stay on your device. Token streaming happens inside the browser tab. Conversation history, if you keep one, lives in your browser's storage (localStorage / IndexedDB) and is removed when you clear site data.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

3.1 HuggingFace (model search and download)

When you use the in-app HuggingFace search, your search query is sent to huggingface.co/api/models. When you load or install a model, the model file is downloaded directly browser-to-HuggingFace from huggingface.co/<org>/<repo>/resolve/main/<file>.gguf. Amni-Scient does not proxy, log, or cache these requests. HuggingFace's own privacy policy applies to those requests; see HuggingFace Privacy.

3.2 Custom URL or Local File Loading

If you load a model via custom URL, your browser fetches it directly from the host you specified. If you load a local .gguf file, bytes never leave your machine.

3.3 Google AdSense (landing page only)

The Amni-LLM landing page at amni-scient.com/amni-llm.html displays a Google AdSense banner. Google's AdSense SDK may collect cookies and device identifiers per Google's Privacy Policy. The runtime app at /lib/amni-llm/ does not display ads.

4. MODEL OUTPUT DISCLAIMER

Models you choose to load are third-party language models. Their outputs reflect their training data and parameters, not Amni-Scient. We do not review, endorse, filter, or guarantee model outputs. You are responsible for the models you load and how you use the responses, including compliance with each model's own license.

5. CACHED WEIGHTS

To avoid re-downloading large model files, Amni-LLM caches model weights in your browser's IndexedDB after first download. You can remove cached weights by clearing site data for amni-scient.com in your browser settings.

6. CROSS-ORIGIN ISOLATION

Amni-LLM ships a service worker (coi-sw.js) that adds COOP/COEP headers to enable SharedArrayBuffer and multi-thread WASM. The service worker only sets response headers; it does not transmit any data and does not log requests.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Amni-LLM is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no information, no information from any user is processed.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

Since we collect no personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, export, or delete. Clearing site data removes any locally cached weights and conversation state.

9. CHANGES

Updates will be reflected with a new "Last Updated" date.
