Questions

Where is my data stored?
On your device, full stop. Company profile, clients, quotes — everything lives in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to us, ever. One-click Export backup (and restore) lives on your Pro Dashboard — back up to a file you control.
Do the free tools stay free?
Yes — every estimator, the 3D designers, plans, materials pricing and guides stay free for everyone. Pro adds the contractor business layer: branding, rates, quotes, packets, showcases.
What happens when my trial ends?
The free estimators keep working exactly as before. Your Pro data stays on your device and unlocks again the moment you subscribe.
Is there an Android app?
In development — web first, then Android. The web tool already works well on phones and tablets.
