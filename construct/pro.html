What Pro adds

Branded quotes.

Your logo, license number and terms on a print-ready quote. Labor lines are hours × rate. Then materials markup, overhead, discount, tax and deposit. The design's bill of materials flows in itemized. The client sees your price, not the store price.

Permit packet.

One click builds a cover sheet (owner, contractor, parcel, declared valuation) and staples it to the plan sheets the estimator already drew. For a deck that's framing, decking, stringer template, and ledger and connection details.

3D showcase link.

Send the homeowner a link that opens their project in 3D with your company name on it. The whole design rides inside the link itself, so I don't store it anywhere. The link does not include your unit-price table.

Clients and pipeline.

A client book with autofill, named projects per client, and a dashboard of quotes by status and dollars won. Works across all 11 trades: deck, patio, pool, floor, roof, framing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, garden and plan.

Backup.

Export everything to one file. Restore it on another device or after clearing the browser.

What it doesn't do

  • It isn't an engineer's stamp. Span checks and local code notes are guidance. Your building department decides, and some jobs need a licensed engineer. The permit packet is your drawings and your submission.
  • No sync. Data lives in the browser you used. Moving to another device means export, then import. Clear the browser without a backup and it's gone. I can't recover it, because I never had it.
  • No card payments or e-signatures inside the app. Print it, PDF it, sign it the way you do now.
  • Prices are what you enter or paste. Paste a Home Depot or Lowe's product page and the materials list fills in. There's no live price feed.
  • Android app isn't out yet. The web version works on phones today.

Questions

Where is my data stored?
In your browser's local storage on that device. Company profile, clients, quotes, projects. Nothing goes to my server. The one exception is checkout: Stripe handles the payment, and the licence server sees your key and a hashed device id so it can count devices. It never sees a quote.
What happens when the trial ends?
The free estimators keep working exactly as before. Your Pro data stays on the device. It unlocks again the moment you enter a key.
I bought it. Where's my key?
On the page Stripe sends you to right after paying. Copy it somewhere safe. Open any estimator → 💼 Pro → Enter key. Lost it? Email me from the address on your Stripe receipt and I'll resend it.
Does it work offline?
The estimators run in the browser. Activating a key needs a connection once. After that, Pro keeps working offline for up to 7 days between checks.
Can I use it for my whole crew?
One licence covers one contractor on up to 3 devices. Need more? Email me and I'll set up a multi-seat invoice.
Is there an Android app?
In progress. Web first, then Android.
