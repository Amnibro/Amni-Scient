SWIPE • PREDICT • CORRECT — ENTIRELY ON YOUR PHONE

VERSION 0.14.2 • 4.1 MB • ANDROID 8.0+

Amni-Type is a keyboard for Android that swipes, predicts and corrects without anything leaving your phone. Why another keyboard? Because every keyboard sees everything you type: messages, searches, passwords in the wrong field. Most of the good ones send some of that somewhere. I built this one the other way round. The word list, the language model, the corrections and everything it learns about how you type all live on the phone. No account, no sync, no telemetry, and no network permission being used to phone home.

What it does

  • Swipe typing over a 286,000-word vocabulary, including the informal words real people text with, not just dictionary English.
  • Next-word prediction from a bundled language model, plus the pairs it learns from you as you go.
  • Context-aware contractions. It works out let's go from lets him, and it's a from its tail, instead of blindly adding apostrophes.
  • Calibration. A guided pass measures where your thumbs actually land and how loose your swipes are, then tunes the recogniser to you.
  • And the rest: themes, layouts (QWERTY, AZERTY, QWERTZ, Colemak, Dvorak plus swipe-optimised grids), emoji and kaomoji, clipboard tools, voice input, and handwriting.

ZERO TELEMETRY

No analytics, no account, no sync. The dictionary and the language model never leave the phone.

SWIPE ON-DEVICE

Full-vocabulary glide typing, scored right on the phone. Short sloppy paths still have to earn the word.

SIDELOAD APK

Signed and hosted right here, or join the beta on Google Play or TestFlight. Then pick Amni-Type as your on-screen keyboard.

New in 0.14.2

In landscape the keyboard now sits on the right at about a third of the screen, so one thumb reaches it and the app stays visible beside it. Switch to the left, or back to full width, from the ⋯ panel or Studio. A new ⌄ button hides the keyboard, and the suggestion strip stays out of the way until you start a word. Amni-Type now has its own Android ID, com.amniscient.type, so it installs next to an older copy; once you have switched keyboards, remove the old one.

New in 0.14.0

Swipe is much better at telling near-twin words apart, like gold and good, cold and cool, or night and might. The decoder now checks every key the word needs, in order, and the corners your finger actually turned, and its weights were tuned on real swipes from a real phone. Your own most-used words count for more, too. Typing and swiping get optional touch effects, and auto-capitals, double-space period and word learning each have their own switch.

Installing it

It’s a normal Android APK, signed and distributed directly instead of through the Play Store. Download it, open the file, and if Android asks, allow your browser to install apps. Then enable it in Settings → System → Languages & input → On-screen keyboard, and switch to it with the keyboard button. Android will warn you that any keyboard can collect what you type. It shows that warning for every keyboard. This one has nowhere to send it.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. It’s free, and there’s nothing to buy inside it.
