Amni-Type

SWIPE • PREDICT • CORRECT — ENTIRELY ON YOUR PHONE

DOWNLOAD APK HOW TO INSTALL

Every keyboard sees everything you type — messages, searches, passwords in the wrong field. Most of the good ones send some of that somewhere. Amni-Type is built the other way round: the word list, the language model, the corrections and everything it learns about how you type all live on the phone. There is no account, no sync, no telemetry, and no network permission being used to phone home.

What it does

  • Swipe typing over a 286,000-word vocabulary — including the informal words real people text with, not just dictionary English.
  • Next-word prediction from a bundled language model, plus the pairs it learns from you as you go.
  • Context-aware contractions — it works out let's go from lets him, and it's a from its tail, instead of blindly adding apostrophes.
  • Calibration — a guided pass that measures where your thumbs actually land and how loose your swipes are, then tunes the recogniser to you.
  • Themes, layouts (QWERTY, AZERTY, QWERTZ, Colemak, Dvorak plus swipe-optimised grids), emoji and kaomoji, clipboard tools, voice input, and handwriting.

Installing it

It is a normal Android APK, signed and distributed directly rather than through the Play Store. Download it, open the file, and allow your browser to install apps if Android asks. Then enable it in Settings → System → Languages & input → On-screen keyboard, and switch to it with the keyboard button. Android will warn you that any keyboard can collect what you type — that warning is shown for every keyboard, and the honest answer here is that this one has nowhere to send it.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. Free, and there is nothing to buy inside it.
