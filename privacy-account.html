Amni Account Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026  ·  Last updated 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Amniscient, LLC (“Amniscient”, “we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Amni account at account.amni-scient.com: one optional sign-in for amni-scient.com apps, starting with Amni-Stitch. Each app’s own policy says what that app sends; this page covers the account itself.

Related documents: product privacy index · Amni-Stitch privacy · Terms.

1. Signing in

You sign in with Haven or with Google, and you can link both to one account. We never see your Haven or Google password.

Sign-inWhat we receive
HavenYour Haven username, display name and a link to your profile picture
GoogleGoogle’s identifier for your account, your email address, your name and a link to your profile picture

2. What the account keeps

CategoryHandling
Your name and picture linkTaken from your sign-in; you can change the name on the account page
Email addressOnly when you sign in with Google
Saved app dataOnly what you choose to save from an app, such as Amni-Stitch measurements when you press Save to account, with its name and date
Access keys you createA fingerprint of each key (not the key itself), its first few characters, its label, and when it was created and last used
Sign-in cookieOne cookie on account.amni-scient.com that keeps you signed in for 30 days; Sign out everywhere ends every session. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

3. Requests and logs

Apps that offer the account, such as Amni-Stitch, ask the account service whether you are signed in when they open. That request carries the sign-in cookie if you have one and, like any web request, your IP address and browser user-agent. The account service runs on our own server, reached through Cloudflare, and it does not write requests to a log.

4. Licenses

If you hold an Amni-CAD Pro license issued to the same email address as your account, the account page shows its plan, seats, issue date and expiry. The license itself is covered by the product’s own policy.

5. Staff access

Our staff can see each account’s name, email address, sign-in methods, creation date and how many saved sets and keys it has on an admin page, to handle support and licenses; they can turn an account off, sign it out or delete it. Staff do not see the contents of your saved data on that page.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Signing in is optional; every app that offers it works without an account.
  • On the account page you can download everything the account holds, change your name, sign out on every device, revoke keys, delete saved data or delete the account.
  • Account records are kept until you delete them or the account.
  • For anything else, write to hello@amni-scient.com.

7. Children

The Amni account is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above.
