CHAT RELAY

RUN IT YOURSELF • YOUR SERVER, YOUR DATA • IT CAN’T READ YOUR MESSAGES

HOW TO RUN IT SETUP GUIDE THE APP

Run the server Amni-Chat talks to yourself, and point the apps at it instead of ours.

  • Delivers sealed messages, calls and push wakeups
  • Stores ciphertext, public keys and hashed identifiers, never message content
  • One command to install, with a free https address, automatic certificates and a built-in TURN server for calls
  • Sign in with Haven for your Haven community
  • Works with no Google or Firebase at all

WHAT IT DOES

Amni-Chat on iPhone and the web, and Android unless it’s off-grid, goes through a relay. The apps use ours at chat.amni-scient.com by default. Run your own and the metadata stays on your hardware.

Messages are encrypted on the devices before they reach the relay. It passes them along and can’t open them.

DELIVERY

Live delivery over a WebSocket, and a store-and-forward inbox for devices that are offline.

DISCOVERY

Find people by phone number or email. The relay keeps a keyed hash, and lookups are blinded so it doesn’t learn who was searched for.

CALLS

Call setup, plus a built-in TURN server for calls that can’t connect phone to phone. It forwards encrypted media it can’t decrypt.

WEB APP AND DEVICES

Serves the Amni-Chat web app from your own domain, keeps linked devices in sync, and holds encrypted attachments for 30 days.

PUSH, YOUR WAY

A connected app gets messages live with no push service. For wakeups, turn on UnifiedPush (Google-free), WebPush, or your own Firebase project. All optional.

SIGN IN WITH HAVEN

Point it at your Haven server and members sign in with their Haven account and find each other by @name. Make the relay members-only with one setting.

CIPHERTEXT ONLY

It stores sealed messages until they’re delivered, public keys, hashed identifiers and timestamps. No message content, no analytics.

RUN IT

Any computer that stays on: a spare PC, a Mac, a Raspberry Pi or a small cloud server. No domain, port forwarding or certificates needed. On Linux or Mac, paste this in a terminal:

curl -fsSL https://downloads.amni-scient.com/amni-chat-relay/install.sh | sh

LINUX AND MAC

The line above. It installs, starts on boot and prints your https address.

WINDOWS

Download, unzip, double-click Start Relay.bat.

DOCKER

Load the image and run it with one data volume. The guide has the two lines.

POINT THE APPS

Paste the address into Amni-Chat on Android or iPhone. The web app is already at that address.

Want a permanent address with your own domain, Sign in with Haven, or the full list of settings? It’s all in the setup guide.
