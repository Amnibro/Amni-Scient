Amni-Phone

A PHONE FROM PARTS YOU CAN BUY • TRUE-SIZE HOUSING • IN THE BROWSER

OPEN THE STUDIO PRESETS PRIVACY

Amni-Phone is the studio for a phone a small team could actually order. You pick real parts, with a maker, a price and a datasheet, and the frame fits itself around the screen. The 3D view is the housing at true size. When the stack is honest, you export the BOM, the KiCad project and the shell drawing. Designs stay in this browser.

SOURCED PARTS

Each role shows buyable, RFQ and reference parts. Price, outline and the date it was checked sit on the card.

TRUE-SIZE LAYOUT

Drag parts in 3D or on the board. Collisions, antenna keep-outs and the front-camera hole are checked as you move them.

EXPORT

BOM, placement, a KiCad project and the housing drawing. Auto-route needs the local helper. The studio itself does not.

Presets

Every preset is a stored design, not a sketch with made-up thickness. The foldables marked reference use an OEM panel that a small shop cannot buy as a loose part.

  • Amni Duo and Amni Tiles. Sealed pieces that snap on magnet edges. Twins, a primary with a companion, or a 2 × 2 grid.
  • Slabs. Amni One, Amni Max, Amni Air, Amni Bench, Amni Rugged, Amni Handset.
  • Books and flips. Amni Book, Amni Square, Amni Flip, Amni Keypad. Center hinge or the dual outer hinge.
  • Reference studies. Amni Edge, Amni Slim, Amni Fold, Amni Clamshell.
