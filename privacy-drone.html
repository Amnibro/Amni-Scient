Amni-Drone privacy

Amni-Drone is a design studio that runs in your browser. Published by Amniscient, LLC. Contact: privacy@amni-scient.com.

  • No account and no upload. The page does not send your design to Amni-Scient. There is no analytics and no ad network.
  • Nothing is stored. Edits live in the open tab and are gone when you close it. Nothing is written to local storage or cookies.
  • Exports (the parts list, the drawing, the frame STL and the assembly file) are made in your browser and saved only where you choose.
