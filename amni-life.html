SEE YOUR LIFE

Every fragment is a node — a person, place, event, work, idea, or era. Every relationship is an edge. Switch between seven layouts to find the shape that fits the question you’re asking. Drop a folder of photos and watch the map populate from EXIF dates and GPS. Encrypt the result and share with one person, or no one.

Open directly in your browser — no account, no install required (but installable as a PWA).

DEFAULT

CONSTELLATION

Force-directed 3D graph. Connected fragments pull together; lone ones drift. The shape your life makes when you let the relationships speak first.

CHRONO

TIMELINE

A clean horizontal line by year. Same-year fragments stack vertically. Useful when you want the story to be linear for a moment.

POETIC

SPIRAL

Golden Fibonacci spiral by date. Earliest at the core, today at the outer arm. Decades become rings.

TYPOLOGICAL

CLUSTER

Six clouds, one per kind — people, places, events, works, ideas, eras. Pulls patterns into focus.

EPOCHAL

RADIAL

Concentric era rings — childhood at the center, this year at the edge. Easy to scan how big each era was.

GEOGRAPHIC

GLOBE

3D wireframe sphere. Geo-tagged fragments project onto the surface; ones without coordinates float in a ring beneath the south pole.

DENSITY

CALENDAR

Year × month grid. Same-month fragments stack. Surfaces seasons-of-life and the years that were heavy or empty.

BUILT TO LAST OFFLINE

🛡

LOCAL-FIRST

LocalStorage for fragments. IndexedDB for photos, videos, voice notes. URL hash + Web Crypto for encrypted shares. Service Worker caches the entire app shell. After your first visit, Amni-Life works with no network.

⚙

RUST / WASM ENGINE

Five layout algorithms compiled to WebAssembly: timeline, force-directed constellation, golden-spiral, cluster, radial. Runs once on load — ~5 ms for 25 fragments, scales to thousands.

📷

PHOTOS & PEOPLE

Drop a folder of photos — Amni-Life parses EXIF for dates and GPS, attaches each photo to a new fragment. Drop a Google Takeout export — sidecar JSON metadata flows in too. Connect Google Photos directly via your own OAuth (BYO Client ID, your data, your account).

🔒

ENCRYPTED SHARES

Compress + encrypt your map with a passphrase. AES-GCM 256, PBKDF2-SHA256 with 200k iterations. The result is a URL hash — nothing leaves your browser. Recipient unlocks with the passphrase. Plaintext shares also available.

🎤

VOICE + TOUR

Record voice notes directly in-browser via MediaRecorder. Hit TOUR to walk through your life chronologically; toggle NARRATE and the browser’s speech synthesis reads each fragment aloud as you fly between them.

📊

UNDO & PATH-FINDER

Every edit is undoable / redoable (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y). Alt-click between any two fragments to BFS the shortest path through your connection graph. Multi-select for batch tag and delete.

SETUP & INSTALL

Three ways to use Amni-Life. Pick the one that fits.

1. Open in browser
Visit amni-scient.com/amni-life. After first visit, the Service Worker caches the app, and you can use it offline forever.
2. Install as PWA
Click the install icon in your browser address bar (or use the “INSTALL” button in the top-right of the app). Amni-Life becomes a native-feeling app on your taskbar / home screen.
3. Self-host (one-liner)
On Linux / macOS: curl -fsSL https://amni-scient.com/amni-life/install.sh | bash On Windows: iwr -useb https://amni-scient.com/amni-life/install.ps1 | iex Both download v0.26.0 to ~/amni-life and start a local server.

Or just download the v0.26.0 zip and unzip it anywhere — double-click index.html and you’re running.
