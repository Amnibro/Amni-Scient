Amni-Photos privacy

Amni-Photos backs up photos and videos from a phone to a photo library that runs on a computer its owner controls. Amniscient, LLC does not run a cloud photo service and does not sell or share anyone's photos or details.

What the phone app reads

Your photo library, so it can back up photos and videos. The camera, only to scan the pairing QR code; nothing it sees is saved. On Android, permission to show a notification while a backup runs.

What the phone app sends, and where

Photos, videos and their details (date taken, the place if the camera saved one, file name and size) go only to the library address you pair the phone with. Over the internet that connection is encrypted with HTTPS. A library owner can also let phones reach it directly on a home network, where the connection may not be encrypted. The app keeps the library address and a device key on the phone so it can keep backing up.

Signing in

To open the gallery on the web or in the app you sign in with Haven or with Google. With Google we receive your email address and Google's confirmation that the address is verified. We use it only to know which library and shared albums you may open. We never see your Google password, contacts or other Google data. A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.

Who can see your photos

The library owner, who runs the computer it is stored on, and anyone you share an album with. Nothing is public unless you share it.

Deleting your Amni-Photos account and data

Your Amni-Photos account is your library and the Haven or Google sign-in linked to it.

To delete your whole account

  • If you run your own library, delete its folder on your computer. Everything is gone at once: photos, videos, albums, shares, device keys and sign-ins.
  • If someone else runs your library (for example a family member), ask them to remove you with photos people <folder> -yourname and delete your library folder.
  • If your library is at photos.amni-scient.com, email privacy@amni-scient.com from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We delete your library, its photos and videos, your shared albums, your device keys and your sign-in link within 30 days and confirm by email. We keep nothing afterward.

To delete some of your data

  • Deleting a photo or video in the gallery moves it to Recently Deleted; deleting it there removes it for good.
  • Stopping a shared album removes everyone else's access to it.
  • Disconnecting a phone in the app (More, then Disconnect This Phone) removes its device key. Uninstalling the app removes the library address and device key from the phone.

What we do not do

No analytics. No ads or advertising identifiers. No crash reports. No selling or sharing of data.
