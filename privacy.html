THIS WEBSITE: COOKIES & ADVERTISING

This section covers amni-scient.com itself: the calculators, construction guides, learning pages, games, blog, product pages, and the beta tester sign-up. The per-product policies above cover the apps.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.

Consent

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are shown a consent message from Google's certified consent management platform before advertising cookies are used, and can accept, refuse, or change that choice at any time from the privacy settings link the message leaves on the page. If you refuse, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which use cookies only for frequency capping, aggregated reporting, and fraud prevention. Visitors in US states with privacy laws can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information through the same Google message where it applies.

Children

The games inside Amni-Learn show no ads. The Amni-Learn category pages written for younger children (Pre-K, Life Skills, Subject Quizzes, Elementary, STEM Labs, Retro Arcade, Casual) request ads with Google's child-directed tag, which turns off interest-based ads and remarketing on those pages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The site has no accounts, and its only form, the beta tester sign-up below, is for people 13 and older.

Beta tester sign-up

The beta page has a form for people who want to test our apps before they are released. It is the only place on the site where you give us personal information, and only if you choose to.

What we collect: the email address you enter, the apps you pick, whether you use Android, iPhone or both, the optional name or handle you type, and the time you signed up. Nothing else. We do not store your IP address. To stop automated abuse, the sign-up service counts requests per network using a one-way, salted hash of the IP address that expires automatically within an hour and cannot be turned back into the address.

Why: to add your Google account to the tester list for the apps you picked on Google Play, and to contact you about the test, for example if we need to confirm your address or tell you about a new iPhone beta you asked for. We do not use it for newsletters or marketing, and we never sell it.

Who else sees it: for Android testing, the email is entered into the tester list in Google Play Console, and Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy to give that account access to the test. Sign-ups are stored in a database on Cloudflare (Workers and D1), which processes them on our behalf under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. No one else receives them. The iPhone TestFlight links need no sign-up; if you use them, Apple handles your TestFlight participation under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it: until testing of the apps you signed up for ends, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. To delete your sign-up or ask what we hold, email amnibro7@gmail.com from, or mentioning, the address you signed up with. We will also remove you from the Google Play tester lists.

Unsubscribing: every tester email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also reply "stop". To make sure we never email you again, we keep only your email address, how you unsubscribed (link, reply or bounce), whether you asked to leave app testing, and the day, with no time of day, IP address or device details. Unsubscribing also deletes your sign-up. Signing up again, or the resubscribe button, removes the entry.

What the site itself stores

The site has no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no accounts. A few settings are kept in your browser's local storage and never sent to us: the light/dark theme choice, the Amni-Calc disclaimer acknowledgment, calculator unit and layout preferences, estimator projects, learning-game scores, and game saves. Clearing site data in your browser removes them.

Hosting

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. The beta sign-up form above is the one exception: it sends what you enter to our sign-up service on Cloudflare. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and abuse prevention, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs.
