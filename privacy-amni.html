Privacy Policy

App: Amni: Tiny Robot Adventure for Android (package com.amniscient.amni) and iPhone and iPad (bundle ID com.amniscient.amni). Effective: November 15, 2026. Publisher: Amniscient, LLC. Contact: privacy@amni-scient.com.

No data collected

Amni collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it never connects to the internet. It plays fully offline.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress. The pebbles and Glimmer you hold, the collectibles you have picked up, the robot colors you own and your settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device.
  • Device backups. If you back up your device, the system may include the app's data in that backup. We never see it.

Permissions

  • Amni does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or your device's location services.
  • Vibration is used for short buzzes on hits and landings, and you can turn it off in Settings.

Children

Amni is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.
  • We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete. Questions are welcome at privacy@amni-scient.com.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or share personal data. The app sends nothing anywhere.

Changes

If a future version changes what data the app sends, for example to add in-app purchases through Google Play or the App Store, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date above, and the store privacy labels will change to match.
