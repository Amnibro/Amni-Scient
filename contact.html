WHAT TO INCLUDE

Calculator results that look wrong. Send the page URL, every input value with its unit, the result you got, and the result you expected with its source (a handbook table, a code clause, a hand calculation). Confirmed errors get fixed on the live page.

Bug reports. Name the product and version, your browser or device, and the steps that trigger the problem. A screenshot helps. Games and WebGPU tools also need your GPU and browser version.

Privacy requests go to privacy@amni-scient.com. Most tools on this site keep everything in your browser, so there is usually nothing on our side to export or delete. If a product does hold data about you (for example a Braid licence or a store purchase), say which product and it will be handled. Each product's policy is listed on the privacy page.

An ad you think breaks the rules. Ads on this site are served by Google AdSense. Send the page URL and a screenshot of the ad, or use the ad's own "Why this ad?" / report link. AdSense lets us block ad categories and specific advertisers.

Security issues. Email security@amni-scient.com privately before disclosing anything publicly, and include enough detail to reproduce it. The security page covers response times and safe harbor.

Business, licensing, press. Licensing questions for AmniTex are covered on the licensing page; anything else can go to hello@amni-scient.com.

ELSEWHERE

Release notes and product news are posted on the blog (also available as RSS). Common questions are answered in the FAQ. Background on the studio and the developer is on the About page. If you want to support the work, there is a Ko-fi page.
