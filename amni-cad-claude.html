Design Parts with Claude

FREE BROWSER CAD • YOUR AI DRIVES IT • REAL STRESS NUMBERS BACK

Ask most chatbots for a part and you get a paragraph, or a block of code you have to paste somewhere and hope it runs. I wanted to say “make the bracket 20% lighter and show me the stress” and actually watch it happen. So Amni-CAD lets your assistant drive the CAD program directly.

What your assistant can actually do

The bridge gives your assistant the same tools you have in the app, not a toy subset:

  • Build and edit real solids. Parametric features on an OpenCascade kernel, with undo for every change.
  • Check its own work. It gets screenshots and automated design-review findings back, so it sees what it made.
  • Run the analysis. Stress (FEA), natural frequencies, and wind-tunnel flow, solved on your GPU, with peak stress, displacement, safety factor and drag returned as numbers.
  • Get it made. STEP and STL export, flat DXF, shop quotes, and CNC G-code.

Why a bridge instead of a sign-in

Websites can’t sign in to a Claude Pro/Max or ChatGPT Plus plan. Those plans only work inside their own apps. So Amni-CAD goes to your assistant instead. The bridge is one small file that runs on your own computer and only listens on 127.0.0.1. It speaks MCP, the standard way AI apps connect to tools, so it works with Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex, Gemini CLI, opencode and Cursor. Your subscription stays with your assistant. Amni-CAD never sees your account, your prompts or your key.

Set it up in about five minutes

  • Install Node.js 18 or newer. Nothing else; there’s no npm install.
  • Open Amni-CAD, go to the AI settings and choose Local bridge. It shows a one-line setup for your system (it adds the bridge to Claude Code) and a config snippet for every other assistant.
  • Ask your assistant to “check the Amni-CAD connection.” It gives you a six-digit code. Type it into Amni-CAD once and that browser stays paired.
  • Keep Amni-CAD on screen and ask for what you want in plain words.

Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Safari blocks websites from talking to apps on your own computer. If you’d rather not use a bridge at all, the built-in assistant takes a Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or OpenRouter key, or runs a model fully on your device.

What it costs

Amni-CAD is free to use in your browser, with no install and no sign-up. Pro is $15/month and adds things like the headless API. The AI side costs whatever your assistant already costs you.
