A QUOTE YOU CAN CHECK

Amni-CAD prices a part from its geometry while you design it. This page shows one real run, start to finish, so you can judge the numbers before you open the app. Every figure below came from the app’s own Instant Quote on the Manufacture tab, run in a headless browser on October 15, 2026, using the default shop rates. Those defaults are a first-pass US starting point, not a price list. The section on your own shop rates shows how to replace them.

THE PART

  • Source: the built-in Mounting plate template, with width 6 in (152.4 mm), depth 4 in (101.6 mm) and thickness 0.125 in (3.175 mm)
  • Features: four 0.281 in (7.14 mm) corner holes and 0.25 in (6.35 mm) corner radii. The template’s 0.02 in (0.5 mm) top chamfer is deleted, because a chamfer makes the app treat the plate as a machined part instead of a cut sheet.
  • Material: aluminum 5052-H32
  • Quantity: 25, standard lead time, no finish
  • Detected as: flat sheet, so laser and waterjet are quoted

DFM FLAGS, PINNED TO THE PART

Design-for-manufacturing checks run with the quote. Each flag names the process, the limit and the number that broke it, and a marker appears on the 3D part at the spot to fix.

To show a flag, the same plate gets one deliberately bad feature: a 0.03 in (0.76 mm) vent hole at the center. The app’s result for each process:

  • Fiber laser: hole 0.03 in (0.76 mm) is below the 0.062 in (1.59 mm) minimum; it may not cut cleanly, so drill or ream it after. Severity: warning.
  • Abrasive waterjet: the same hole is below the 0.07 in (1.78 mm) minimum.

The price moves with the change: the laser quote becomes $3.57 each ($89.36 for 25) and the waterjet quote $4.74 each ($118.55 for 25), because there is one more pierce and more cut length. The shop file also holds minimum web, slot gap and part-size limits for every process, and the app checks those the same way.

The warning appears under Design for Manufacturing. In the 3D view an amber marker sits on the hole.

YOUR OWN SHOP RATES

The defaults are there so a first quote works on day one. They are labeled as a first pass, and the app says to calibrate them before quoting customers. Choose Shop rates… on the Manufacture tab to edit the whole cost model as a text file, in inches, inches per minute, dollars per pound and dollars per square foot. It is saved in your browser only and never sent anywhere. Reset restores the defaults.

WHAT ELSE A SHOP GETS

G-CODE

GRBL, LinuxCNC and Fanuc-style posts with linted G-code, tabs, helical holes and ramped entries, for machines from a Genmitsu 3018 up to a Haas VF-2.

DRAWINGS

Automatic ANSI drawings in decimal inches with fits and GD&T, exported as PDF, SVG or DXF. Pro adds your own sheet formats, built from a drawing PDF you already use.

STEP IN, STEP OUT

Import a customer’s STEP file, quote it, and send back a STEP, STL, 3MF or DXF. Nothing is uploaded; the quote is computed on your computer.
