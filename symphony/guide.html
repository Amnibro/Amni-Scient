EVERYTHING SYMPHONY DOES — FAMILIES, CALLS & THE COMMUNITY

💻 OPEN THE WEB APP ⬇ DOWNLOAD THE ANDROID APP

Symphony is two things in one private app: a Family space — invite-only, end-to-end encrypted chat, photos, voice and video calls for the people closest to you — and a Community — a public, encrypted place everyone can join, with Reddit-style topic groups. It runs on Android and in any web browser, and the two talk to each other. No phone number, no email, no ads, no tracking.

1 · Getting started

On your phone (Android)

  1. Tap Download the Android app above and install symphony.apk. (Android may ask you to allow installing from your browser — that's normal for apps outside the Play Store.)
  2. Open Symphony and type your name — that's all the sign-up there is. No phone number or email.
  3. To talk with family: tap Start My Family to create one, or Join My Family → scan the QR code (or paste the link) someone sent you.
  4. The 🌐 Community is already there — tap the globe in the top bar any time.

On a computer (web)

  1. Open symphony.amni-scient.com in any browser.
  2. Type your name. If a family member sent you an invite link, paste it and tap Join family.
  3. No invite yet? Tap 🌐 Browse the Community to jump straight into the public Community.
Your identity is a private key created on your device the first time you open Symphony. Keep using the same device/browser to stay "you." There's nothing to remember and no password to lose.

2 · Families (private & invite-only)

A Family is a small, private group — only people you invite can see it, and every message is end-to-end encrypted.

  • Create one: type your name → Start My Family.
  • Invite people: open the family, tap the name at the top → Invite. Show the QR code in person, or tap Share link to send it by text/email. The link is encrypted to your family — only they can use it.
  • Two ways to talk: the # General chat for back-and-forth messages, and the # Feed for posts everyone sees (photos, updates, events).
  • Share anything: 📷 photos, 🎤 voice messages, 📎 files, ✨ stories (disappear in 24h), 📅 events, and 📊 polls.

3 · The Community (public & encrypted)

The Community is one big space everyone on Symphony shares — like a town square. It's still end-to-end encrypted (only people with the app can read it; the relays that carry it cannot), but anyone can join and post.

  • Open it: tap 🌐 in the top bar (phone) or the 🌐 Community button (web). You're auto-joined — no invite needed.
  • Post: type at the bottom and send. Share photos too with 📷.
  • React: tap a post → pick 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🔥. Tap a reaction chip to add/remove yours.
  • Reply: tap a post → Reply; your message shows a quote of what you replied to (tap it to jump back).
  • See who's who: tap someone's name or avatar for a quick profile (their recent posts here).
  • Keep it comfortable: tap a post → 🔇 Mute this person to hide everything from them (🔊 Unmute any time). It's private to you.
  • New activity: the 🌐 button shows a dot when there are new posts; inside, a ↓ button jumps you to the latest.

4 · Sub-communities (topic groups, like subreddits)

Don't want to see everything at once? Join focused topic groups inside the Community.

  • Join or create one: in the Community, tap ＋ topic (web) or 🧩 Join or create a sub-community (phone) and type a topic — e.g. gardening, austin-tx, recipes.
  • Anyone who types the same topic name lands in the same encrypted space — that's how you find each other. There's no central list; the name is the key.
  • Switch between Main and your topics with the chips at the top.
  • Share a topic: open it → Share link, and whoever opens it joins that exact sub-community.
Suggested starters appear when you explore: Gardening, Recipes, Tech, Sports, Music, Books, Pets, Travel, Faith, Parenting. Tap one to join in a tap.

5 · Chat features (everywhere)

Both Families and the Community share a rich set of messaging tools:

  • Reply to a specific message, react with emoji, @mention people, and pin important messages.
  • Save (⭐) messages for later and view your saved-only list.
  • Search a conversation, forward a message, edit or delete your own, and copy text.
  • Photos open in a full-screen viewer with a download button; tap-and-hold a message for its menu; swipe a message to reply (touch).
  • Half-typed messages are saved as drafts per conversation, and unread chats show a badge.

6 · Choose your look

Symphony can wear a whole different feel. Pick one when you first open the app, or change it any time — your messages, families and Community stay exactly the same; only the look changes.

  • 🎼 Classic — Symphony's cozy, calm default.
  • 📘 Facebook — clean blue-on-grey; opens on your Feed.
  • 🎮 Discord — dark and chat-first, with a compact message list.
  • 📸 Instagram — bright and photo-first; opens on your Feed.
  • 💬 Messenger — bright blue with big rounded chat bubbles.
  • 🌙 Midnight — a true-black timeline, easy on the eyes.
  • On your phone: a chooser appears the first time you open Symphony. Change it later in Settings → Choose your look.
  • On the web: pick one on the welcome screen, or tap 🎨 at the top (or Settings → Choose your look) for the Appearance picker.
  • Accent colour: on top of any look you can still choose an accent colour (blue, green, purple and more) in the same Appearance picker.
Some looks open straight to your Feed (Facebook, Instagram, Midnight) and others to your Chats (Discord, Messenger, Classic) — matching the app they're inspired by. It's just a starting point; switch tabs any time.

7 · Voice & video calls

Inside a Family, tap 📞 at the top to start a call. Calls are peer-to-peer and encrypted, with voice, video and screen-sharing. (Calling isn't offered in the public Community — it's for the people you've invited.)

8 · Privacy & encryption

  • End-to-end encrypted. Family messages and calls are encrypted so only members can read/hear them. Community posts are encrypted to a shared key that every Symphony app has — so the public relays that pass messages along only ever see scrambled data, never your content.
  • No accounts, no tracking. No phone number, no email, no ads, no analytics. Your identity is a key on your device.
  • Families are private (invite-only, not discoverable). The Community is public — anyone with the app can read and post there, so treat it like a public square.
  • You're in control: mute anyone in the Community, leave a family any time, and clear a chat from your device.

9 · Installing & staying updated

  • Android: download symphony.apk from this site and install it. Symphony updates itself — when a new version is available it offers to download and install it, so you stay current without the Play Store.
  • iPhone / iPad: open symphony.amni-scient.com in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen for an app-like experience.
  • Computer: just use symphony.amni-scient.com — nothing to install.

10 · Tips & troubleshooting

  • "I don't see the Community" — tap the 🌐 globe (top bar on phone, header on web). You're already a member.
  • Invites: if an Open in Symphony link doesn't open the app, install the app first, then tap the link again.
  • Same person, two devices? Each device has its own identity, so you'll appear as a separate member. Use one device per "you" for now.
  • Photos in the Community are kept small so they reach everyone reliably — for full-resolution sharing, use a Family.
  • Looks & colours: tap 🎨 (web) or open Settings → Choose your look to switch between the Facebook, Discord, Instagram, Messenger and Midnight looks — and to pick an accent colour.
