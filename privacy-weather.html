AMNI-WEATHER — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: July 17, 2026  |  Last Updated: July 28, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Amni-Weather, a browser-based weather map application published by Amniscient, LLC under the Amni-Scient brand ("we", "us", "our"), handles user information.

1. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect no personal data on Amni-Scient servers. Amni-Weather does not require accounts, registration, email, or payment. We do not operate an application backend that receives weather-probe coordinates, search queries, or session state from your browser.

2. DATA HANDLING

Field processing (gridding, smoothing, colormap rasterization, unit conversion) executes in your browser via WebAssembly and JavaScript. Map rendering runs locally on your GPU/CPU. Preferences (last place, units, UI choices) may persist only in your browser via localStorage (e.g. amni-wx-prefs-v1) and are not transmitted to us.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

3.1 Open-Meteo (live + point forecast)

The application requests forecast, marine, air-quality, and geocoding data from Open-Meteo public APIs (api.open-meteo.com, marine-api.open-meteo.com, air-quality-api.open-meteo.com, geocoding-api.open-meteo.com). Requests include map-region or pin coordinates and selected meteorological variables. They do not include your name or account identifiers. Open-Meteo’s own policies govern their processing; see open-meteo.com.

3.1b Map tiles (basemap)

Basemap imagery is fetched directly by your browser from public tile hosts matching the Amni-Drive stack: Esri World Imagery, OpenStreetMap, CARTO basemaps, and OpenTopoMap (depending on the basemap you select). Tile requests include zoom/x/y only. Those providers’ terms and privacy policies apply to their tile services.

3.1c Hazards (NWS + GDACS)

Alert overlays may fetch public hazard feeds from the US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) and GDACS global severe-event feeds. Requests are coordinate- or region-scoped public API calls; we do not receive those responses on Amni-Scient servers.

3.2 Optional browser geolocation and IP approximate locate

If you allow it, the browser may share your approximate location with the page solely to center the map. That permission is controlled by your browser; we do not receive that coordinate on our servers. If geolocation is denied or fails, the page may call a client-side IP geolocation service (e.g. geojs / ipwho-style public endpoints) to estimate a rough city-level center — still browser-direct, not via our servers.

3.3 Static libraries

Map and UI libraries are preferably served from amni-scient.com (including vendored assets under /weather/). If a CDN is used for a dependency, standard HTTP requests for static scripts occur; we do not control CDN logs.

3.4 Google AdSense (hosting pages)

Marketing pages on amni-scient.com may display AdSense ads. Google may process cookies and device data per Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive that data.

4. PACK / FALLBACK FIELDS

Baked field packs and silent live-to-pack fallback are processed locally. They do not invent a separate cloud account path. Live API calls still follow section 3 when active.

5. DATA SHARING

We do not sell or trade user data. Network destinations initiated by the weather app itself are the Open-Meteo, tile, hazard, and optional IP-geo endpoints described above (when those features are used) plus static asset hosts.

6. DATA RETENTION

We retain no Amni-Weather user data because we collect none server-side. Clearing site data removes local caches.

7. SECURITY

Core field algorithms ship as compiled WASM. The app runs in the browser sandbox. Use HTTPS when loading from amni-scient.com.

8. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

Amni-Weather is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

Since we store no personal Amni-Weather profiles, there is no server-side data to export or delete. Manage third-party and browser permissions in your browser settings.

10. CHANGES

We may update this policy; the Last Updated date will change. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance.
