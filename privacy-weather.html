AMNI-WEATHER — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: July 17, 2026  |  Last Updated: July 17, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Amni-Weather, a browser-based weather map application developed by Amni-Scient ("we", "us", "our"), handles user information.

1. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect no personal data on Amni-Scient servers. Amni-Weather does not require accounts, registration, email, or payment. We do not operate an application backend that receives weather-probe coordinates, search queries, or session state from your browser.

2. DATA HANDLING

Field processing (gridding, smoothing, colormap rasterization, unit conversion) executes in your browser via WebAssembly and JavaScript. Three.js rendering runs locally on your GPU/CPU. Preferences you set in the UI may persist only in your browser (e.g. theme on marketing pages via localStorage) and are not transmitted to us.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

3.1 Open-Meteo (live mode)

When Live mode is enabled, the application requests forecast and geocoding data from Open-Meteo public APIs (api.open-meteo.com, geocoding-api.open-meteo.com). Requests include map-region coordinates and selected meteorological variables. They do not include your name or account identifiers. Open-Meteo’s own policies govern their processing; see open-meteo.com.

3.1b Map tiles (basemap)

Basemap imagery is fetched directly by your browser from public tile hosts matching the Amni-Drive stack: Esri World Imagery, OpenStreetMap, CARTO basemaps, and OpenTopoMap (depending on the basemap you select). Tile requests include zoom/x/y only. Those providers’ terms and privacy policies apply to their tile services.

3.2 Optional browser geolocation

If you allow it, the browser may share your approximate location with the page solely to center the map. That permission is controlled by your browser; we do not receive that coordinate on our servers.

3.3 CDN libraries

Three.js may load from a public CDN (jsDelivr). Standard HTTP requests for static scripts occur; we do not control CDN logs.

3.4 Google AdSense (hosting pages)

Marketing pages on amni-scient.com may display AdSense ads. Google may process cookies and device data per Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive that data.

4. DEMO MODE

Demo mode synthesizes weather fields locally and does not call Open-Meteo. If a live fetch fails, the app may switch to demo automatically.

5. DATA SHARING

We do not sell or trade user data. The only network destinations initiated by the weather app itself are the Open-Meteo endpoints described above (when live/search is used) and static asset hosts.

6. DATA RETENTION

We retain no Amni-Weather user data because we collect none server-side. Clearing site data removes local caches.

7. SECURITY

Core field algorithms ship as compiled WASM. The app runs in the browser sandbox. Use HTTPS when loading from amni-scient.com.

8. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

Amni-Weather is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

Since we store no personal Amni-Weather profiles, there is no server-side data to export or delete. Manage third-party and browser permissions in your browser settings.

10. CHANGES

We may update this policy; the Last Updated date will change. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance.
