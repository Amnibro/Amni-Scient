HEDGEDOC FOR ANDROID — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 2, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 2, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the unofficial HedgeDoc Android client published by Amniscient, LLC ("we", "us") handles information.

1. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect nothing. There is no Amni-Scient account, no crash reporter, and no analytics SDK. The APK does not phone home to amni-scient.com. We do not operate a HedgeDoc server for this app.

2. WHAT STAYS ON THE PHONE

The server URL, login cookies or API token, theme choice, pin list, and a cache of the last downloaded note live in the app’s private storage on the device. Clearing the app data or using Forget on the connect screen drops the session.

3. NETWORK

The app talks only to the HedgeDoc instance you type in (or demo.hedgedoc.org if you use the public demo). Notes, history, and credentials go to that operator. Their privacy policy governs what they store. Sideload and update checks are files you download from GitHub Releases; the app does not auto-update.
