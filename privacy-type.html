AMNI-TYPE — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: August 23, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 23, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Amni-Type, an Android keyboard published by Amniscient, LLC ("we", "us"), handles information.

1. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect nothing. There is no account, no sync, no crash reporter, and no analytics SDK. The APK does not phone home. We do not operate a server that receives what you type.

2. WHAT STAYS ON THE PHONE

The word list, the bundled language model, swipe traces used for recognition, next-word predictions, and any words you opt to learn locally live in the app’s private storage on the device. Learned words are off until you turn that setting on.

3. NETWORK

Amni-Type does not send keystrokes, swipe paths, or predictions to amni-scient.com or anywhere else. Optional voice input, if you enable it, uses the speech recogniser already on the phone and stays under that component’s policy.

4. ANDROID’S KEYBOARD WARNING

Android shows the same “this app can collect what you type” notice for every third-party IME. That notice is the OS, not a confession from us. This keyboard has nowhere to send that text.
