Amni-Companion

Models

Your models

Use any rigged humanoid .glb or .vrm (Mixamo, VRoid, Ready Player Me, Unreal, Blender Rigify, Amni-CAD…). Files stay in this browser.

AI brain

The built-in offline brain knows a few dozen things and works with no network. For real conversation, connect any AI service with your own key. Your key is saved only in this browser and sent only to the service you pick, directly.

Settings

Camera AR and senses

Recognition runs entirely on this device with MediaPipe. No video or images leave it, unless you ask a connected AI brain what it sees; then it gets one snapshot.

Voice

Privacy

No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Models, settings and keys live in this browser's storage. Voice input uses your browser's speech service, which may run in the cloud (Chrome uses Google; Safari uses Apple) unless your browser supports on-device recognition.

About

Amni-Companion by Amniscient. Free software under the AGPL-3.0. Source code. Its core (rig mapping, body tracking, gesture recognition, offline brain) is written in Helium. Credits: three.js (MIT), MediaPipe (Apache-2.0), Quaternius Universal Animation Library (CC0).
