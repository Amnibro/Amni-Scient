Amni-Mail privacy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

Amni-Mail is a desktop client that runs on your computer. Amniscient, LLC does not operate a mail server for it and does not receive your messages.

Sharing

Amniscient, LLC does not receive your Google mail, tokens, or credentials, and does not sell or share that data. You can disconnect the account in Amni-Mail and revoke access in your Google Account’s third-party app settings.

What stays on the machine

Message bodies, headers, attachments, contacts, calendar events, rules, and search indexes live in a local SQLite database in the app’s data folder on your computer. Passwords and OAuth tokens are stored in an encrypted credentials file keyed on that machine.

What leaves the machine

IMAP and SMTP traffic goes to the mail host you configure (Google, Microsoft, Fastmail, and so on). When you connect a Google account, sign-in and token exchange go to Google. Optional AI features call only the URL you set (the default is a server on your own machine). If you point that URL at a third-party service, that service receives the prompts those features send.

The app may contact amni-scient.com for a version file and to download updates. That path does not include your mail or credentials. The install script and download zip are static files.

Google accounts

When you sign in with Google, Amni-Mail uses Google’s OAuth to request access to your Gmail account (the https://mail.google.com/ scope). That permission lets the app read, send, and manage your mail over IMAP and SMTP, the way a desktop mail client does.

  • Access: The app talks directly to Google for sign-in, tokens, and mail. Amniscient’s servers are not in that path.
  • Use: Mail is shown in the local Amni-Mail window so you can read, search, organize, and send. Amniscient does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or training models.
  • Storage: As above — mail in a local database, tokens in an encrypted credentials file on your computer.
