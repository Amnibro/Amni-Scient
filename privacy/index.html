Amni Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-20

Amni is built privacy-first.

What we collect

  • By default: nothing is sent for analytics, ads, or crash telemetry.
  • Saves stay local (desktop files or browser storage / export codes).
  • Optional local crash log only if you enable it (AMNI_CRASH_LOG / --crash-log) — not uploaded automatically.
  • Optional telemetry flag is reserved and currently does not transmit data.

Steam

Valve may process data under Steam's privacy policy. Steam Cloud (when enabled) stores only Amni save blobs under an AmniV2 path.

Web

Host may process standard access logs for security. No ad networks in the game shell. Play at /game/v2/.

Children

Family-friendly target (E10+ / PEGI 7). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
