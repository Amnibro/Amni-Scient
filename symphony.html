ONE APP FOR THE PEOPLE WHO MATTER

Symphony is a private place for your family or community to share and talk — a feed, group chats, calls, photos, stories and polls, all end-to-end encrypted. There's no account to make and no company in the middle: you create a Family, invite people with a link or QR, and your messages route peer-to-peer through a relay that only ever sees ciphertext. Simple enough for grandma, private by design.

📱

A FEED, NOT A FEED ALGORITHM

Open straight into your people's posts — photos, updates, polls. No ads, no ranking games, no strangers. Just the families and communities you chose.

💬

CHAT, CALLS & ROOMS

Group and 1:1 messaging, voice/video calls and screen-share, channels per Family, replies, reactions, GIFs, stickers and voice notes.

🔒

END-TO-END ENCRYPTED

One Ed25519 + X25519 identity per device (X25519 → HKDF → ChaCha20-Poly1305). The relay forwards sealed envelopes it cannot read. No plaintext, ever.

🎉

STORIES, POLLS & EFFECTS

24-hour stories, create-and-vote polls, link previews, double-tap reactions, swipe-to-reply, and confetti/heart bursts on the moments that matter.

🎨

FULL-PALETTE THEMING

Make it yours, MySpace-style: whole-app color themes (Dracula, Nord, Daylight & more) with a live preview, plus custom chat backgrounds.

🔗

NO ACCOUNT, DECENTRALIZED

No sign-up, no phone number. Create a Family and share an invite link or QR. Built to run on a relay, a phone, or mesh — your network, your rules.

INSTALL IN THREE STEPS

  1. Tap Download APK above. When your browser warns about the file type, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded symphony.apk. Android will ask to allow installs from your browser — tap Settings → enable Allow from this source, then back out and tap Install.
  3. Open Symphony, pick a name, and create or join a Family. That's it — updates download in-app automatically.

Symphony is distributed as a sideloaded app (it isn't on the Play Store). It's debug-signed by Amni-Scient; you can update it any time from inside the app.
