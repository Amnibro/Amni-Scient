These 2K equirectangular maps replace procedural texture generation
for known Solar System bodies in AMNI-EXPLORE.

KTX2 maps (7.6.0)

  Scope maps above, re-encoded (CC BY 4.0, same attribution).

  Public-domain NASA / USGS / NOAA products, re-projected, resampled and
  encoded for AMNI-EXPLORE (no endorsement implied):
    earth.ktx2      NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, August 2004 (NASA Earth Observatory / Reto Stockli)
    earth_ht.ktx2   NOAA NGDC ETOPO5
    moon.ktx2       LRO LROC WAC colour mosaic (NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit)
    moon_ht.ktx2    LRO LOLA LDEM 16 ppd (NASA PDS Geosciences Node)
    mars_ht.ktx2    MGS MOLA MEGDR 16 ppd (NASA PDS)
    venus_ht.ktx2   Magellan GTDR global topography 4641 m (USGS Astrogeology)
    io.ktx2         Galileo SSI / Voyager global colour merge 1 km (USGS Astrogeology)
    europa.ktx2     Voyager / Galileo SSI global mosaic 500 m (USGS), greyscale tinted
    ganymede.ktx2   Voyager / Galileo SSI global mosaic 1 km (USGS), greyscale tinted
    callisto.ktx2   Voyager / Galileo SSI global mosaic 1 km (USGS), greyscale tinted
    enceladus.ktx2  Cassini ISS global mosaic 110 m (USGS), greyscale tinted
    titan.ktx2      Cassini ISS P19658 global mosaic 4 km (USGS), greyscale tinted

  Height maps are 8-bit, quantised between the lo/hi values in manifest.json.
  Everything else in the Solar System view is computed.
