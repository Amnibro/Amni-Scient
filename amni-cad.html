Do my designs leave my computer?

No. Geometry, simulations and drawings are computed in your browser, and designs are saved only there or in files you download. There is no account for the free tier and no activity logging. Read exactly what the optional cloud features send.

Which CAD programs can I exchange files with?

Any program that reads STEP: SOLIDWORKS, Inventor, Fusion, Creo, NX, CATIA, Solid Edge, Onshape and FreeCAD. Drawings export as PDF, SVG and DXF, and meshes as STL, 3MF, OBJ, glTF and PLY.

Does it work offline?

Yes. License keys are checked on your device. For a machine that is never online, contact us about a fixed-term or perpetual key.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from Pro → Manage billing in Amni-CAD. Your key works to the end of the paid period, then everything in Free keeps working.
