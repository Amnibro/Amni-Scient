Waystone

CLIMB • MINE • BANK • SUMMIT

ANDROID: SIGN UP FOR THE APK IPHONE: JOIN ON TESTFLIGHT

Free beta for Android and iPhone. Co-op with friends on the same network.

Waystone is a co-op climbing roguelite. Every expedition is a new mountain: a seeded, procedurally generated massif rising out of the sea, ringed by floating islands, sea spires and stone bridges. Climb it hand over hand, manage your grip, carry ore up the faces and decide when to bank it at a campfire and when to risk it all for the greed bonus at the summit. Light the beacon and everyone wins. Fall, and you lose what you were carrying.

FOUR ZONES, ONE CLIMB

Saltcliff Shore, Mossback Gorge, The Rime and Cinder Crown, each with its own weather, from rockfalls and flash floods to biting cold.

GRIP, COLD AND HUNGER

Grip drains as you hang on. Injuries, cold and hunger eat into it, so every route up a bad wall is a choice.

CHESTS, SHRINES AND RELICS

Buy keys before the climb, crack chests on the way up, bargain at shrines and stack relics, from real jackpots to the occasional rubber duck.

CLIMB TOGETHER

Lower a rope to a friend, revive the fallen and share the cost: revived climbers are soul-linked until the next campfire.

More to discover

  • Bank or push your luck. Bank ore at a campfire to keep it, or carry it to the summit for two and a half times the haul.
  • Day and night. Night comes cold and dark, and something walks the mountain. Stay near a fire.
  • Tools and loadouts. Spend banked ore at Base Camp on tools, tool mods and body upgrades. Everyone carries a glider.
  • Grades. Push to harder grades for richer ore, better chests and new modifiers.
  • Music by place. Each zone has its own day and night tracks that drift in now and then while you climb.

Join the beta

One tap joins: TestFlight on iPhone, Google Play on Android. On Android, the first time, join the testers group, then tap Become a tester. Climb solo or join a friend’s room. To play together, everyone joins the same Wi-Fi or a phone's hotspot, one player hosts, and the others join. Questions? See support.
