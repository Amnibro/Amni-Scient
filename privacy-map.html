Amni-Map Privacy Policy

App: Amni-Map for Android (package com.amniscient.map). Effective: September 29, 2026. Publisher: Amniscient, LLC. Contact: amnibro7@gmail.com.

Amni-Map is a navigation app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts or any device or advertising ID. This policy explains what the app sends, to whom, and why.

Location

Amni-Map uses your phone's location while the app is open and while a trip you started is running (shown by the "Amni-Map is guiding you" notification). It does not use location in the background at any other time.

To give you routes, search results, traffic, speed limits and place details, the app sends coordinates to the Amni-Map gateway, a server we run on Cloudflare. For routes, search, speed limits and place details the gateway forwards only the request itself (coordinates or search text), never your IP address or phone details, to public OpenStreetMap-based services: the OSRM, FOSSGIS and Valhalla routing servers, Photon (komoot) and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) for search, and public Overpass servers for map details. The gateway does not keep request logs.

On your phone only

  • Home, Work, saved places, recent searches, settings, the last drive's route line, cached map and traffic data, and the traffic speeds your phone has learned stay on your phone. Uninstalling or clearing the app's storage deletes them.
  • The music map (Pro) reads the beat and loudness of the sound your phone is playing, using Android's audio visualizer, only while music is playing and a music mode is on. Android files that API under the microphone permission. It is analysed on your phone as it plays; nothing is recorded, stored or sent.
  • Voice search: when you tap the microphone in search (on the phone or in Android Auto), your words go to your phone's speech recognition service, which runs on the phone when it can (otherwise your phone's speech provider, such as Google, processes them under its own privacy policy). Amni-Map receives only the text, which it uses like typed search text. Amni-Map never records, stores or sends audio. In Android Auto the car's microphone is used for the same thing.
  • The on-phone copilot (Pro) uses Gemini Nano through Google's ML Kit on your phone. Your question does not leave the phone.
  • If you tap Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT in the copilot section, Amni-Map opens that app with a short text (destination name, next turn, time left, traffic and speed). That app's privacy policy applies to what happens next. Amni-Map never uses or stores API keys or logins for those services.
  • When other Amni-Scient apps signed by us are installed, Amni-Map can share your last position with them on the phone, so they can offer "navigate there" shortcuts. This never leaves the phone.

Purchases

Amni-Map Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Amni-Map only receives Google Play's confirmation that your subscription is active, and it keeps that on the phone.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate): navigation, search near you, speed limits and traffic.
  • Foreground service (location): keeps turn-by-turn guidance running with the screen off during a trip you started.
  • Notifications: the ongoing navigation notification.
  • Microphone: voice search for places when you tap the microphone, and syncing the music map to the song your phone is playing when a music mode is on. No audio is recorded, stored or sent to us. You can say no and still type searches; the music map then keeps a steady beat.
  • Android Auto: navigation templates and the car map surface.

Children

Amni-Map is a driving app meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Turn off "Help map traffic" in Menu › Driving to stop sending speed data.
  • "Remove my reports" in the report sheet hides your reports on your phone. Reports already shared stay visible to others until they expire.
  • Deny or revoke location permission at any time in Android settings (navigation will not work from your position).
  • Because nothing we store is linked to you, we cannot look up "your" data. If you have a question or want something removed, write to amnibro7@gmail.com with the time and place of a report and we will delete it.

Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. The gateway and our routing, search and map-data servers keep no IP addresses and no request logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.

Saved routes: every route you navigate is saved on your phone in a compact form (the route line, turns, timings and the start and end points) so the app can show it instantly next time and use it when you are offline. Saved routes stay in the app's private storage on your phone, are excluded from Android backups and device transfers, are never uploaded, and are removed automatically when there are more than 500 or 50 MB of them. You can delete them all in Menu › Route › Saved routes.
