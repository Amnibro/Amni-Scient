Amni-Map Privacy Policy

Apps: Amni-Map for Android (package com.amniscient.map) and Amni-Map for iPhone and iPad (bundle ID com.amniscient.map). Effective: October 8, 2026 (added the iOS app). Publisher: Amniscient, LLC. Contact: amnibro7@gmail.com.

Amni-Map is a navigation app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking or advertising SDKs. It does not collect your name, email, phone number or contacts, and neither the app nor any library inside it sends a device ID or advertising ID. This policy explains what the app sends, to whom, and why.

The sections from "Location" to "Permissions" describe the Android app. The iPhone and iPad app is described in Amni-Map for iOS. "Children", "Your choices", "Security" and "Changes" apply to both.

Location

Amni-Map uses your phone's location while the app is open and while a trip you started is running (shown by the "Amni-Map is guiding you" notification). It does not use location in the background at any other time, and it never shares your location with other apps on your phone.

To give you routes, search results, traffic, speed limits and place details, the app sends coordinates to the Amni-Map gateway, a server we run on Cloudflare. Routes, search and speed-limit lookups use your exact position. The gateway relays only the request itself (coordinates or search text), never your IP address or phone details, to public OpenStreetMap-based services: the OSRM, FOSSGIS and Valhalla routing servers, Photon (komoot) and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation) for search, and public Overpass servers for map details. Those services see the gateway, not your phone, and keep their own logs of what the gateway asked. The gateway itself keeps no request logs.

On your phone only

  • Home, Work, saved places, recent searches, settings, the last drive's route line, cached map and traffic data, and the traffic speeds your phone has learned stay on your phone. Uninstalling or clearing the app's storage deletes them. If you use Android's own device backup, Android includes the app's settings, Home, Work, saved places and recent searches in your backup; we never see it.
  • Saved routes: every route you navigate is saved on your phone in a compact form (the route line, turns, timings and the start and end points) so the app can show it instantly next time and use it when you are offline. Saved routes stay in the app's private storage, are excluded from Android backups and device transfers, are never uploaded, and are removed automatically when there are more than 500 or 50 MB of them. You can delete them all in Menu › Privacy & data › Saved routes.
  • The copilot (Pro) builds its tips on your phone from your route, next turn, time left, traffic delay, speed and the speed limit. No AI service is used and nothing about your drive goes to one. To name gas, coffee and rest stops ahead, it asks the gateway for places within 300 m of the next 25 km of your route, the same kind of map question as a category search.
  • If you tap Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT in the copilot section, Amni-Map opens that app with a short text (destination name, next turn, time left, traffic and speed, no coordinates). That app's privacy policy applies to what happens next. Amni-Map never uses or stores API keys or logins for those services.
  • The music map (Pro) reads the beat and loudness of the sound your phone is playing, using Android's audio visualizer, only while music is playing and a music mode is on. Android files that API under the microphone permission. It is analysed on your phone as it plays; nothing is recorded, stored or sent.
  • Voice search: when you tap the microphone in search (on the phone or in Android Auto), your words go to your phone's speech recognition service, which runs on the phone when it can (otherwise your phone's speech provider, such as Google, processes them under its own privacy policy). Amni-Map receives only the text, which it uses like typed search text. In Android Auto the app passes the car microphone's sound straight to that recognizer. Amni-Map never stores audio or sends it to us.
  • Spoken directions use your phone's text-to-speech engine. Voices marked "needs data" in Menu › Voice & sound are online voices: the spoken text goes to your speech engine's provider (such as Google). The default voice and the other voices run on the phone.

Purchases

Amni-Map Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. Google Play Billing talks to the Play Store app on your phone; its built-in usage logging to Google is switched off in Amni-Map. Amni-Map only receives Google Play's confirmation that your subscription is active, and it keeps that on the phone. Purchase details are never sent to us.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate): navigation, search near you, speed limits and traffic. No background location permission.
  • Foreground service (location): keeps turn-by-turn guidance running with the screen off during a trip you started.
  • Notifications: the ongoing navigation notification.
  • Microphone: voice search for places when you tap the microphone, and syncing the music map to the song your phone is playing when a music mode is on. No audio is recorded, stored or sent to us. You can say no and still type searches; the music map then keeps a steady beat.
  • Android Auto: navigation templates, the car map surface and the car's parked state.
  • Google Play billing: buying and restoring Pro.

Amni-Map for iOS (iPhone and iPad)

The iOS app has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking or advertising SDKs. It contains no Google code, and neither the app nor the MapLibre map library inside it sends a device ID or advertising ID. It uses the same Amni-Map gateway as the Android app, and the gateway handles its requests the same way: no request logs, no IP addresses stored, and the caching and retention described in the table above.

Location

Amni-Map asks only for While Using the App location permission; it never asks for "Always". While a trip you started is running, iOS keeps sending the app location updates with the screen off or in another app, so spoken guidance continues, and iOS shows the blue location indicator while it does. When the trip ends, the app stops using location in the background. Spoken turn prompts use the background audio mode only to play while you navigate. The app never shares your location with other apps on your device.

The iOS app does not have the Android app's "Help map traffic" speed upload, tester codes, copilot, music map, voice search, Android Auto or Pro subscription, so none of the data those features use is collected on iOS. It does not use the microphone or the camera.

On your device only

  • Home, Work, saved places, recent searches and settings are kept in the app's preferences. Up to four saved places also appear as Home Screen quick actions.
  • Saved routes: routes you navigate (up to the 60 most recent) are saved in the app's private storage so they open instantly and work offline. They are never uploaded. Clear them, and recent searches, in Menu › Privacy & data.
  • Spoken directions use Apple's built-in speech synthesizer on the device.
  • If you share your arrival time, the app hands the destination name, arrival time and an openstreetmap.org link for the destination to the app you choose in the iOS share sheet. "Report missing" opens openstreetmap.org in Safari at your position so you can leave a map note there, under the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. Phone numbers and websites of places open in the Phone app or Safari.
  • Deleting the app removes all of this. If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include the app's preferences and saved routes in that backup; we never see it.

Permissions

  • Location While Using the App: your position on the map, routes from where you are, search near you, speed limits and turn-by-turn guidance.
  • Background location and audio (iOS background modes, not a separate permission): used only while a trip you started is running, to keep guidance and spoken prompts going.

App Store privacy label

Data collected, none of it linked to you and none used for tracking, all for app functionality: precise location (route points, search and lookup positions, traffic areas and report locations), search history (search text sent to find places), other user content (road reports and votes) and customer support (feedback text). Data used to track you: none.

Children

Amni-Map is a driving app meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Turn off "Help map traffic" in Menu › Privacy & data to stop sending speed data.
  • Clear saved routes and recent searches in Menu › Privacy & data.
  • On iOS, turn off Live traffic in the menu to stop loading the traffic overlay tiles.
  • "Remove my reports" in the report sheet (Android) hides your reports on your phone. Reports already shared stay visible to others until they expire.
  • Deny or revoke location permission at any time in Android settings or the iOS Settings app (navigation will not work from your position).
  • Because nothing we store is linked to you, we cannot look up "your" data. If you have a question or want something removed, write to amnibro7@gmail.com with the time and place of a report and we will delete it.

Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. The gateway keeps no request logs and stores no IP addresses. To stop abuse, Cloudflare's rate limiter counts posted reports, votes, feedback and tester codes per IP address for one minute and then forgets them. The public services the gateway relays to keep their own logs, which show the gateway, not you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
