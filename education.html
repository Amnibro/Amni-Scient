Who is covered?

A seat is one instructor or one lab computer. A site license covers the students and instructors in that program, on the school’s machines, for coursework and shop projects.

Is this the $5 student plan?

No. The $5 student plan is one student buying Amni-CAD for their own learning. A school license is sold to the school.

Can a shop class use Amni-Construct?

Yes. Deck, patio, framing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and the other estimators are part of the same quote as Amni-CAD. The free estimators stay free for anyone. The school license is the right to run them as a class.
