Privacy Policy

App: Waystone for Android (package com.amniscient.waystone) and iPhone and iPad (bundle ID com.amniscient.waystone). Effective: October 24, 2026. Publisher: Amniscient, LLC. Contact: amnibro7@gmail.com.

Data Not Collected

Waystone does not collect, send or share any information about you with us or anyone else. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking and no advertising or third-party SDKs. We run no server for it.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress. Your banked ore, tools, upgrades, settings and climb history are saved in the app's own storage on your device.
  • The game's own little server. To run, the app starts a small web server that only your device can reach. It serves the game's files and your save to the game itself.
  • Device backups. If you back up your device, the system may include the app's data in that backup. We never see it.

Playing together

Co-op connects your device straight to your friends' devices over your local network or a phone's hotspot. What passes between you is game data: climber names you type, positions and what happens in the climb. It goes only to the players in that room, never to us. On Android, hosting opens the room to devices on the same network until you stop hosting, and shows a notification while it's open.

Permissions

  • Network and Wi-Fi state (Android) and local network (iPhone): to find and join friends' rooms on the same network.
  • Notifications (Android): only to show that you're hosting a room.
  • Waystone does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Children

Because Waystone collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete. Questions are welcome at amnibro7@gmail.com.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that sends any data, such as ads in the Google Play version, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date above, and the store privacy labels will change to match.
