Privacy Policy

App: Waystone for Android (package com.amniscient.waystone) and iPhone and iPad (bundle ID com.amniscient.waystone). Effective: October 27, 2026. Publisher: Amniscient, LLC. Contact: amnibro7@gmail.com.

Ads by Google AdMob

Waystone itself collects nothing about you: no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting of its own, and we run no server for it. The Android and iPhone apps show ads from Google AdMob, whose SDK collects the data described below. The Ad-free subscription turns the ads off.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress. Your banked ore, tools, upgrades, settings and climb history are saved in the app's own storage on your device.
  • The game's own little server. To run, the app starts a small web server that only your device can reach. It serves the game's files and your save to the game itself.
  • Device backups. If you back up your device, the system may include the app's data in that backup. We never see it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows a full-screen ad when it starts and one when you leave a climb's results screen, never during a climb. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To deliver, measure and protect ads, the SDK may collect and share with Google: your device's IP address (which can be used to estimate a general location), your advertising ID or app set ID on Android or an app-scoped device identifier on iPhone, app interaction data such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic information about app and ad performance. Data is encrypted in transit. Waystone never asks to track you across other companies' apps on iPhone. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it, including the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent choices through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads load. You can change them at any time under Settings, Store, Ad choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases

Runes, the game's optional premium currency, come in packs, and Ad-free is an optional monthly subscription that removes the ads and adds Runes each month. Both are sold through Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the store which purchases your account has, adds the Runes from a pack to your balance on your device, and remembers whether Ad-free is active. You can manage or cancel Ad-free under Settings, Store, Manage, or in your store account's subscriptions.

Playing together

Co-op connects your device straight to your friends' devices over your local network or a phone's hotspot. What passes between you is game data: climber names you type, positions and what happens in the climb. It goes only to the players in that room, never to us. On Android, hosting opens the room to devices on the same network until you stop hosting, and shows a notification while it's open.

Permissions

  • Network and Wi-Fi state (Android) and local network (iPhone): to find and join friends' rooms on the same network.
  • Notifications (Android): only to show that you're hosting a room.
  • Advertising ID (Android): lets the ads SDK read your advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings.
  • Billing (Android): lets the app offer the Ad-free subscription through Google Play.
  • Waystone does not use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or your device's location services.

Children

Waystone is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.
  • Subscribe to Ad-free to stop the ads and the data the ads SDK collects with them.
  • We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete. Data the ads SDK sends goes to Google; see Google's Privacy Policy for your choices there. Questions are welcome at amnibro7@gmail.com.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal data. Apart from the ads and store purchases above, the app sends nothing anywhere.

Changes

If a future version changes what data the app sends, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date above, and the store privacy labels will change to match.
