Common questions

Where is my grove saved? On your device only. The game saves on its own about once a minute and whenever you leave the app, and it keeps a few backup copies. If a save is ever damaged, the game loads the newest good backup.

Can I move my grove to a new device? Yes. iCloud Backup or a computer backup includes the game's data, and the save folder also shows up in the Files app under the game's name, so you can copy it yourself.

Do I need an internet connection or an account? No. The game works fully offline and has no accounts or ads.

What happens while I am away? Time in the grove keeps going gently while the app is closed, up to a limit, so your Pomlings will have grown a little when you come back. You can change the pace in Settings.

The game feels warm or slow on my device. Turn off shadows or turn on battery saver in Settings. The game also lowers its own effects when the device gets hot.

Text is hard to read, or I play with my left hand. Settings has text size, reduce motion, captions, haptics and left-handed trays.
