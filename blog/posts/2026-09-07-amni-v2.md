# Amni v2 plays in the browser

I wanted the remake in the tab, no installer, so [Amni v2](/game/v2/) is the one I have been shipping on. You play Amni, a robot about the size of a bolt, bringing color back to thirteen realms off a clock-ring hub.

This week I put lives and life pickups in, sealed the boss exits, gave each realm its own enemy models, and pushed the worlds toward the Banjo look: fatter trees, snowcaps, saturated skies. WebGPU, gamepad ready, saves stay on this device.

You need a browser with WebGPU. Classic is still at [/game/](/game/).
