# Amni v2 plays in the browser

You play Amni, a robot about the size of a bolt, bringing color back to thirteen realms off a clock-ring hub. [Amni v2](/game/v2/) is the version I've been shipping on.

This week I put in lives and life pickups, sealed the boss exits, and gave each realm its own enemy models. I also pushed the worlds toward the Banjo look: fatter trees, snowcaps, saturated skies.

It runs on WebGPU, it's gamepad ready, and your saves stay on this device. You do need a browser with WebGPU. Classic is still at [/game/](/game/).
