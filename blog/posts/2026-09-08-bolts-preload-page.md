# The bolts preload page

I wanted the bolt page to match the tool I actually open. [calc/bolts.html](/calc/bolts.html) is torque and preload: nut factor K, thread and face friction, yield check, scatter.

The relation is still `T = K·D·F`. VDI 2230 and ISO 898-1 are the citations on the page. There is a worked flange number in the notes if you want to see the arithmetic once, then you jump to the calculator.

Stress and fatigue sit next door when the joint is not just a torque problem. Same tab, same local kernels.

If you already know your K and your target preload, skip the notes and hit the launch button.
