The bolts module page is getting the human pass: title and deck that say torque & preload, a subhead that does not waste a scroll, FAQs that answer nut-factor K without a keyword pile, and a clear jump into the calculator.

Under the hood the WASM path is the same idea as always — T = K·D·F when you use the nut-factor form, friction-split when you have μ, preload against proof load × stress area. K is not universal; lubrication and finish move it more than material folklore.

If the PR is still landing, you may see copy catch up a day behind the brief. The calculator at [/calc/](/calc/) `#tab-bolts` (and [/calc/bolts.html](/calc/bolts.html)) is the part that matters for numbers.

Not FEA. Not a substitute for VDI 2230 / ISO 898-1 judgment on a critical joint. A fast local check when you already know what question you are asking.
