# A swipe keyboard that stays on the phone

Every keyboard sees what I type, and most of the good ones send some of it somewhere, so I wanted swipe, next-word, and autocorrect that never leave the phone.

Amni-Type keeps the 286,000-word list, the language model, and the pairs it learns from me on the device. There is no account, no sync, and no network permission used to phone home. It can tell *let's go* from *lets him* instead of blindly adding apostrophes, and there is a calibration pass that measures where my thumbs actually land.

Sideload the APK, then pick it under Settings → System → Languages & input. Android will warn you that a keyboard can collect what you type, and that warning is for every keyboard. This one has nowhere to send it.

Android 8 or newer. Free, nothing to buy inside it. The page is [amni-type.html](/amni-type.html).
