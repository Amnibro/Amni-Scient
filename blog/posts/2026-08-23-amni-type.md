# A swipe keyboard that stays on the phone

Every keyboard sees what you type, and most of the good ones send some of it somewhere. I wanted the good stuff without that part.

So everything stays on the phone: the 286,000-word list, the language model, and the pairs it learns from you. There's no account, no sync, and no network permission used to phone home.

It's smart about the little things too. It can tell *let's go* from *lets him* instead of blindly adding apostrophes. And a calibration pass measures where your thumbs actually land.

Setup is quick. Sideload the APK, then pick it under Settings → System → Languages & input. Android will warn you that a keyboard can collect what you type. That warning shows up for every keyboard. This one just has nowhere to send it.

Android 8 or newer. Free, with nothing to buy inside it. Grab it from [amni-type.html](/amni-type.html).
