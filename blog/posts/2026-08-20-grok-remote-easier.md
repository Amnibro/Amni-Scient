# Grok Remote is easier to live with

First up, the chat list. On a wide screen, old chats were getting cut off. Now the left rail lists every conversation, and you can scroll it. Rail hidden? The ☰ in the chat header brings it back.

Next, reactions. I wanted to react to a reply without a row of emoji sitting on every bubble. Now a long-press opens a picker, and the ×, a tap outside, or Escape closes it. No more stuck bar.

And if I cancel a message I already queued, the text comes back into the box instead of vanishing. Tiny fix, huge relief!

Oh, and the header has a small bench drawing of Rikku, because she's who I talk to in there.
