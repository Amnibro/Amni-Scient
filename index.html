WHAT WE BUILD & WHY

Every Amni-Scient project exists because existing solutions fell short in privacy, transparency, or accessibility. Rather than accept those tradeoffs, we built alternatives from scratch.

Engineering calculators are usually either expensive desktop suites or imprecise web forms. Amni-Calc runs FEA beam analysis, seal design, bolt torque, and thermal calculations in a WASM backend — interactive, accurate, and free, with theory and worked examples on every module.

Educational platforms for children commonly track behavior, serve ads, or require accounts. Amni-Learn collects zero data, runs offline after loading, and covers Pre-K through college prep plus adult brain training with 60+ interactive games and baked natural voice.

Web browsers ship with telemetry baked in. Amni-Browse is a single Rust binary with zero telemetry, navigation-layer ad blocking, DNS-over-HTTPS, and an AES-256-GCM password vault — everything local, nothing phoned home.
