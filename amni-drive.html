ZERO-ADB SETUP

Up and running in your car in under two minutes using only your phone.

  1. Install Amni-Drive (Amni-Map Included!)

    Download and install Amni-Drive — Amni-Map is prebuilt right inside the APK, so one tap on the welcome wizard or in-app banner installs live 3D maps immediately (even offline).

  2. Let Amni-Drive Claim USB

    Open Settings → ADVANCED → AMNI WIRE and tap Open Android Auto App Info → Disable (or Clear defaults) so stock Android Auto doesn’t steal the cable first. Grant Display over other apps if prompted.

  3. Optional: Sync Web Sign-Ins Once

    Under AMNI WIRE → Car Web Sign-Ins, sign in once to YouTube, Spotify Web, or Netflix on your phone — cookies stay on-device and sync automatically to your car’s Media tile.

  4. Plug In & Drive

    Plug into your car’s Android Auto USB port and tap Always open Amni-Drive. Drag dividers to resize tiles, tap ⤢ for full screen, and pick Left Rail or Bottom-Center Dock anytime.
