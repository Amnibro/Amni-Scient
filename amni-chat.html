Encrypted chat on your phone. No account, and no server of mine in the middle.

  • Text, voice, video and screenshare
  • MLS, via OpenMLS, in a Rust core
  • Nostr when you’re online. A Bluetooth mesh when you’re not.
  • Add someone with a QR code or a link

WHAT IT DOES

I built Amni-Chat because I wanted a messenger that still works when nobody’s server is in the path. It’s an Android app. A conversation can be text, a voice call, video, or a screenshare.

Encryption is MLS, Messaging Layer Security, through OpenMLS. That code sits in a Rust core. There is no account. There is no central server. You add a contact by scanning their QR code or opening their link. That’s the whole introduction.

MLS

End-to-end encryption for the group. OpenMLS does the protocol. The Rust core is what the app actually runs.

NOSTR

Online traffic goes over nostr. Those relays move packets. Plaintext stays inside the MLS session.

BLUETOOTH MESH

Phones in radio range pass traffic over Bluetooth. That hop is the radios talking to each other.

SMS

The other offline path. A text message carries it when the mesh can’t see the other phone.

QR OR LINK

That’s how you add a contact. Scan the code, or open the link.

NO ACCOUNT

The app makes the keys on the phone. There is nothing to sign up for, and nothing of mine you have to log into.

HOW A MESSAGE MOVES

1. KEYS

Open the app. It generates the keys on the device. You don’t register them with me.

2. A CONTACT

Show your QR code, or send your link. They add you. You add them. That pair is the conversation.

3. ONLINE

When both phones have a network, the packets go over nostr. MLS already wrapped them.

4. OFFLINE

Bluetooth mesh if you’re close enough. SMS if you’re not. Same ciphertext, different pipe.

LIMITS

It’s a beta. The Android build installs from an APK while Google reviews the Play test. The beta page also has an iPhone TestFlight link.

Offline means Bluetooth and SMS. It does not mean the internet keeps working with the radio off and nobody nearby.

Skip the relay and you don’t get its discovery, web client, multi-device sync, or push. Chat still works. License is AGPL-3.0.
