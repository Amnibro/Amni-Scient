WHAT IT DOES

Amni-Chat is a messenger for Android, iPhone and the web. Text, photos, files, voice notes, voice and video calls, and screenshare from Android.

Every message is encrypted on your device. The relay in the middle passes sealed envelopes along and can’t open them. Use ours, or run your own and point the apps at it.

END-TO-END ENCRYPTED

X25519 key agreement, HKDF-SHA256 and ChaCha20-Poly1305, with Ed25519 signatures. Each device signs a fresh key every week for forward secrecy. The same code path on Android, iPhone and web. Attachments are encrypted before upload.

FIND PEOPLE PRIVATELY

Look someone up by phone number or email. The lookup is blinded, so the relay matches it without learning who you searched for. It stores a keyed hash, never the number itself. Or sign in with your Haven account and find friends by @name.

CALLS AND SCREENSHARE

Voice and video calls, plus screenshare from Android. Calls are encrypted and connect phone to phone when they can. When they can’t, a TURN relay forwards the encrypted media.

MORE THAN ONE DEVICE

Link another phone or an iPhone to the same account, and your chats follow you.

OFF-GRID ON ANDROID

With no signal and no Wi-Fi, messages hop between nearby Amni-Chat phones over Bluetooth. Android can also send over public nostr relays, so it works without any relay of ours.

SMS TOO

On Android it can text people who don’t have the app yet, so one inbox covers everyone. Those are ordinary text messages, not encrypted.

HOW A MESSAGE MOVES

1. KEYS

The app makes your keys on the device. Only the public half goes to the relay, so friends can reach you.

2. FIND OR ADD

Search by number or email, or scan a QR code or open an invite link in person.

3. SEND

Your phone seals the message. The relay holds the envelope until the other device picks it up. Attachments are deleted from it after 30 days.

4. NO SIGNAL

A message sent during an outage waits in a queue and retries until a path opens: data, the Bluetooth mesh, or nostr.

LIMITS

It’s a beta. The Android build installs from an APK while Google reviews the Play test; iPhone is on TestFlight. Both are on the beta page.

The Bluetooth mesh, nostr and SMS are Android only. iPhone and the web app always go through a relay.

Messages are sealed to keys that each device replaces every week and then erases, so keys stolen later can’t open older messages. There’s no per-message ratchet yet: someone who takes a device can read what it received in the last few weeks.

Encryption hides what you say, not that you’re online: a relay, and your network, can see when your device connects. The license is AGPL-3.0; the source code isn’t public yet.
