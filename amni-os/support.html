Amni OS is made by a small studio. Here is how to get help, and how to make a report we can act on quickly.

Haven communityAsk questions, share fixes, talk to other Amni OS users. GitHub issuesBug reports, hardware requests and feature ideas. TroubleshootingFixes for the problems people hit most.

Before you ask

  • Check troubleshooting and the FAQ.
  • If an update broke something, boot a restore point to get working again while we look.

Haven community

haven.amni-scient.com is where Amni OS users and the developer talk. It is the quickest place for questions like “how do I…” or “is this normal?”.

Report a bug

Open an issue at github.com/Amnibro/Amni-OS/issues and pick a template:

  • Bug report: something is broken. It asks for your amni-os-core version and the amni-bug-info output.
  • Hardware or driver request: a device does not work. It asks for lspci -nn and lsusb.
  • Feature request: an idea.

amni-bug-info

Run it in Konsole and paste the output into your report:

amni-bug-info

It prints a short summary of your system: the Amni OS and amni-os-core versions, the Amni packages, the kernel and boot mode, the graphics stack from /var/lib/amni-os/gpu.json, the driver rules that matched, restore point status, pacman signature settings, the update timer and any failed services. Run it with sudo to include the restore point count.

It does not include your username, hostname, IP or MAC addresses, or serial numbers, and it replaces any that slip into the output. It prints to the screen and sends nothing anywhere. Read it before you post; it is yours to share or not.
