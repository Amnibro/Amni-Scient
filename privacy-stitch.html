Amni-Stitch Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026  ·  Last updated 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Amniscient, LLC (“Amniscient”, “we”, “us”) handles information in connection with Amni-Stitch at amni-scient.com/stitch, which drafts sewing patterns from body measurements and can generate sample pictures of garments.

Related documents: product privacy index · Amni-Stitch · Terms.

1. What stays in your browser

The pattern engine runs entirely in your browser. The following is stored only in your browser’s local storage, and we never receive it unless you choose to save measurements to an Amni account (Section 6):

Your try-on photoKept in the page; sent to us only when you press Make picture (Section 2)

A random device identifierLocal storage; used only by subscriptions, which are not open yet

2. Outfit-sample and try-on requests

Outfit samples need an Amni account (Section 6). When you press Make sample, your browser sends a request to the account service at account.amni-scient.com, which runs on a computer that Amniscient operates, reached through Cloudflare. The request contains:

  • the description you typed, the garment type, the picture style and the fabric type;
  • your sign-in cookie, which tells us which Amni account is asking;
  • your IP address and your browser’s user-agent string, as with any web request.

For a try-on, pressing Make picture also sends the photo you chose (or our model photo), a mask showing where the garment goes, and a short description made from your options, such as color, style, sleeves and fit. Use only a photo of yourself, or of an adult who agreed to it. The photo and mask are stored on that computer only until the picture is finished or the request is dropped, at most about 10 minutes, and are then deleted. We don’t use them for anything else and we don’t train models on them.

The picture is drawn on that same computer by our own image generator (Amni-Gen with the MOSS-Image model) and checked there by our own vision model. No third-party AI provider receives your description, your photo or the picture.

Each account gets 3 free pictures a day, shared between samples and try-ons. We count them against your account. Your IP address is used only in the service’s memory to limit how many samples one network can ask for in a day; it is never written to disk.

3. Safety checks, blocked pictures and bans

Amni-Stitch generates pictures of clothing only. To keep it that way:

  • Requests asking for nudity or sexual content are refused before generation. We keep the refused text with your account, the time and the reason.
  • Try-on photos are checked before anything is drawn. A photo of a child, of more than one person, or with no person in it is refused and not counted. A photo with nudity is refused and counts as a strike; we record your account, the time and the reason, but not the photo.
  • Try-on pictures are checked again before they are shown. One with nudity is not shown, is deleted, and counts as a strike.
  • Every generated sample picture is checked by a vision model on our computer before it is shown. A picture that shows a person is not shown and is deleted. A picture with nudity is never shown; we keep it, with your account, the time and the request text, for review by a person.
  • Strikes and bans. Each refused request and each picture blocked for nudity counts as a strike against your account. Three strikes in 30 days turn outfit samples off for the account. A request that sexualizes a minor is refused and samples are turned off at once. A ban covers every sign-in linked to the account and stays even if you delete the account. You can ask for a review at hello@amni-scient.com.
  • Reporting. If a request or picture appears to involve the sexual exploitation of a child, we report it, including the account details and request text, to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as U.S. law requires (18 U.S.C. § 2258A). NCMEC may share reports with law enforcement. We also respond to valid legal process.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Not open yet. Subscriptions are not open yet, and Amni-Stitch takes no payments. This section describes how they will work; we will update this policy when they open.

Subscriptions are sold through Stripe Checkout. Stripe processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your card number. After payment we receive your name, email address and subscription status from Stripe and issue a signed subscription key. The key contains your name, email address and the end of your paid period in readable form, so treat it like a password and do not post it publicly. Your browser sends the key to our license service to renew it and to open the Stripe billing portal.

6. Amni account (optional)

You can sign in to an Amni account to keep your measurements across devices. The account service runs at account.amni-scient.com on our own server, reached through Cloudflare.

WhatWhat we receive and keep
Signing in with HavenYour Haven username, display name and a link to your profile picture. We never see your Haven password.
Signing in with GoogleGoogle’s identifier for your account, your email address, your name and a link to your profile picture. We never see your Google password.
Saved measurementsOnly when you press Save to account: your measurements, size choices and garment options, the name of the saved set and the date. Nothing else from the page is copied.
Access keys you createA fingerprint of each key (not the key itself), its first few characters, its label, and when it was created and last used.
Sign-in cookieOne cookie on account.amni-scient.com that keeps you signed in for 30 days. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

The Amni account privacy policy covers the account in full. Each time Amni-Stitch opens, your browser asks the account service whether you are signed in. That request carries the sign-in cookie if you have one and, like any web request, your IP address and browser user-agent. The account service does not write requests to a log. If you have an Amni-CAD Pro license under the same email address, your account page lists it. Our staff can see each account’s name, email address, sign-in methods, creation date and how many saved sets and keys it has on an admin page, to handle support and licenses; they can turn an account off, sign it out or delete it.

On the account page you can download everything the account holds, sign out on every device, revoke keys, delete saved measurements or delete the account. Account records are kept until you delete them or the account.

7. Service providers

Cloudflare hosts the page and runs the generation and license services, and processes the request data in Sections 2 to 5 on our behalf. Requests to the Amni account service (Section 6) pass through Cloudflare to our own server. Stripe handles payments as described in Section 5.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can use every pattern, tracing and printing feature, and line up a garment on your photo, without sending us anything: just don’t press Make sample or Make picture.
  • Clearing your browser’s site data deletes everything in Section 1.
  • Signing in is optional. Without an account, nothing in Section 6 is stored, apart from the signed-in check described there, which is not logged.
  • You can ask for access to, or deletion of, the records in Section 4 at hello@amni-scient.com. We may keep safety records, and records we must preserve by law, for the periods above.

9. Children

Amni-Stitch can draft patterns for children’s clothes, but the service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above.
