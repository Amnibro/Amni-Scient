Amni-Stitch Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026  ·  Last updated 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Amniscient, LLC (“Amniscient”, “we”, “us”) handles information in connection with Amni-Stitch at amni-scient.com/stitch, which drafts sewing patterns from body measurements and can generate sample pictures of garments.

Related documents: product privacy index · Amni-Stitch · Terms.

1. What stays in your browser

The pattern engine runs entirely in your browser. The following is stored only in your browser’s local storage, and we never receive it unless you choose to save measurements to an Amni account (Section 6):

CategoryHandling
Body measurements, size choices, garment optionsLocal storage on your device
Photos you upload to traceProcessed in the page; never uploaded
Generated sample pictures, patterns, print pages, sewing guidesKept in the page until you save or close it
A random device identifierLocal storage; sent only with sample requests (Section 2)
Your subscription key, if you have oneLocal storage; sent with sample requests and to the licence service

2. Outfit-sample requests

When you press Generate sample, your browser sends a request to our generation service, which runs on Cloudflare. The request contains:

  • the description you typed, the garment type, the picture style and the fabric type;
  • the device identifier and, if you have one, your subscription key;
  • your IP address, the country Cloudflare derives from it, and your browser’s user-agent string.

The picture is drawn on a computer that Amniscient operates, using our own image generator (Amni-Gen with the MOSS-Image model). No third-party AI provider receives your request.

We count requests per day to enforce the free limit of 3 samples a day. Free requests are counted against a salted hash of your IP address; subscribers are counted against their subscription key.

3. Safety checks, blocked pictures and bans

Amni-Stitch generates pictures of clothing only. To keep it that way:

  • Requests asking for nudity or sexual content are refused before generation. We log the refused text with the IP address, time and reason.
  • Every generated picture is checked by automated nudity detection on our computer before it is shown. A flagged picture is never shown to you. We keep it, together with the IP address, time, country, user agent, device identifier and request text, for review by a person.
  • Strikes and bans. Each blocked picture counts as a strike against the IP address. Three strikes in 30 days ban that address from the generator. A request that sexualises a minor is refused and the address is banned at once. You can ask for a review at hello@amni-scient.com.
  • Reporting. If a request or picture appears to involve the sexual exploitation of a child, we report it, including the IP address and request details, to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as U.S. law requires (18 U.S.C. § 2258A). NCMEC may share reports with law enforcement. We also respond to valid legal process.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold through Stripe Checkout. Stripe processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your card number. After payment we receive your name, email address and subscription status from Stripe and issue a signed subscription key. The key contains your name, email address and the end of your paid period in readable form, so treat it like a password and do not post it publicly. Your browser sends the key to our licence service to renew it and to open the Stripe billing portal.

6. Amni account (optional)

You can sign in to an Amni account to keep your measurements across devices. The account service runs at account.amni-scient.com on our own server, reached through Cloudflare.

WhatWhat we receive and keep
Signing in with HavenYour Haven username, display name and a link to your profile picture. We never see your Haven password.
Signing in with GoogleGoogle’s identifier for your account, your email address, your name and a link to your profile picture. We never see your Google password.
Saved measurementsOnly when you press Save to account: your measurements, size choices and garment options, the name of the saved set and the date. Nothing else from the page is copied.
Access keys you createA fingerprint of each key (not the key itself), its first few characters, its label, and when it was created and last used.
Sign-in cookieOne cookie on account.amni-scient.com that keeps you signed in for 30 days. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

The Amni account privacy policy covers the account in full. Each time Amni-Stitch opens, your browser asks the account service whether you are signed in. That request carries the sign-in cookie if you have one and, like any web request, your IP address and browser user-agent. The account service does not write requests to a log. If you have an Amni-CAD Pro licence under the same email address, your account page lists it. Our staff can see each account’s name, email address, sign-in methods, creation date and how many saved sets and keys it has on an admin page, to handle support and licences; they can turn an account off, sign it out or delete it.

On the account page you can download everything the account holds, sign out on every device, revoke keys, delete saved measurements or delete the account. Account records are kept until you delete them or the account.

7. Service providers

Cloudflare hosts the page and runs the generation and licence services, and processes the request data in Sections 2 to 5 on our behalf. Requests to the Amni account service (Section 6) pass through Cloudflare to our own server. Stripe handles payments as described in Section 5.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can use every pattern, tracing, printing and 3D feature without sending us anything: just don’t press Generate sample.
  • Clearing your browser’s site data deletes everything in Section 1.
  • Signing in is optional. Without an account, nothing in Section 6 is stored, apart from the signed-in check described there, which is not logged.
  • You can ask for access to, or deletion of, the records in Section 4 at hello@amni-scient.com. We may keep safety records, and records we must preserve by law, for the periods above.

9. Children

Amni-Stitch can draft patterns for children’s clothes, but the service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above.
