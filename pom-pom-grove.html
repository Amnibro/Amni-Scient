Pom-Pom Grove

HATCH • RAISE • RACE • DRESS UP

JOIN THE IPHONE BETA ON TESTFLIGHT

Free public beta, iPhone and iPad. Limited spots.

Pom-Pom Grove is a cozy garden where you hatch, raise and cheer on Pomlings: round, cuddly little creatures with a glowing pom-pom that shows how they feel. Feed them by dragging fruit straight from your bag, give them animal parts to change their look, guide them toward bright Dawn or moody Dusk, and take them racing, sparring and playing minigames across five gardens.

RAISE AND CARE

Hatch an egg, then feed, pet and play with your Pomling. Every one has its own voice, traits and favorite fruit.

MIX ANIMAL PARTS

Give a Pomling one of 21 animals and it takes on part of that animal's look and strengths. Mix them for a one-of-a-kind friend.

RACE, SPAR AND PLAY

Grove Races across 13 courses, friendly matches in the Spar Ring, and 12 minigames that train every stat.

FIVE GARDENS

Meadow Grove, Dawn Terrace, Dusk Hollow, Coral Lagoon and Skyreach Garden, each with its own sky, sounds and wildlife.

Screenshots

More to discover

  • Genetics and grades. Seven stats, each graded from E up to S and beyond, plus genes for color and pattern, so careful breeding pays off over generations.
  • Dawn, Noon or Dusk. The fruit you share, the animals you give and how you care for a Pomling shape which evolution tier it reaches. A well-loved Pomling can even be reborn with a head start.
  • Dress up. Hats, scarves, capes, backpacks and toys, with dyes to match. The Night Bazaar has new outfits and treats.
  • Kindergarten. Classes, a Health Center and a Fortune Teller for your Pomlings.
  • Pocket Garden. Take a Pomling along; it earns stars over time and comes home happier and a little stronger.
  • Made for everyone. No ads, no accounts and no internet needed. Your grove is saved on your device with automatic backups. Text size, reduce motion, haptics and left-handed controls are in Settings.
