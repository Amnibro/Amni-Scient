How testing works

A beta is the finished app with the paint still drying. Most of it works, some of it won’t yet, and the only way to find out which is to hand it to people who didn’t build it. You don’t need to be technical. If something confuses you, that’s exactly what I want to hear.

Sign up

On Android, join the testers group with the Google account on your phone. On iPhone, some apps are open right now through TestFlight, no sign-up needed.

Become a tester

Open an app’s Google Play test link and tap Become a tester. Group members are let in right away, no waiting.

Install and use it

Install it from the Play Store like any other app and use it the way you normally would. Updates arrive on their own.

Tell me what you find

Bugs, confusing screens, missing features, things you liked. One sentence is plenty. Screenshots help a lot!

What you get

  • Early access to every app on this page, free.
  • A direct line to the person who builds them. I read the feedback myself. There’s no support queue.
  • New builds the moment they’re ready, before anyone else.

What I ask

  • Keep the app installed and stay signed up for about two weeks. New apps need a steady group of testers before they can launch in the store, so sticking around is the biggest help you can give.
  • Open it now and then, not just once.
  • Send feedback when something breaks or bugs you.

      Questions

      Does it cost anything?

      No. Joining a test is free, and the test versions are free to install.

      Do I have to sign up?

      No. Google Play lets members of the Amni-Scient testers group install the test versions, and anyone can join it. Join with the Google account signed in to the Play Store on your phone. That is usually a Gmail address, but some Google accounts use a different one. The form is optional.

      On iPhone you don’t need to give me anything. The TestFlight links above work for anyone.

      What do you do with my email?

      I use it to add you to the tester list for the apps you picked and to contact you about the test. Nothing else. It isn’t added to a mailing list, sold, or shared with anyone other than the app store that runs the test. The privacy policy has the details.

      How long should I stay in?

      About two weeks, if you can. New apps need a steady group of testers before they can go live in the store, so keeping the app installed and staying signed up for that stretch really matters. You can still leave any time, no hard feelings.

      How do I send feedback?

      On Android, open the app’s page in the Play Store. Testers see a private feedback box there that only I, the developer, can read. On iPhone, take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.

      Or just email amnibro7@gmail.com with the app name, your phone model and what happened.

      Could a test version mess up my phone?

      A test version is the same app with newer code, so it may have bugs or crash now and then. It doesn’t change your other apps or settings, and you can uninstall it whenever you like. When the app launches, your copy simply updates to the store version.

      How do I leave?

      On Android, open the app’s test link again and choose to leave the program, then uninstall if you want. On iPhone, remove the app from TestFlight. To have your email deleted from my list, write to amnibro7@gmail.com.

      What about the other apps on iPhone?

      Amni-Chat, Amni-Learn, Amni-Type and Pom-Pom Grove are open on TestFlight today. Amni-Map, Amni-Crypt, ContAIgion and HumAInity are Android-only for now. Sign up with iPhone selected so I know you’re interested. Their TestFlight links will appear on this page when they open.
