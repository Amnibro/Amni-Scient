Beta test

12 people · 14 days straight

Google Play will not open production for a new app until 12 people have stayed opted into that app's closed test for 14 days in a row. The same 12 people can cover every app. Each person joins each app and stays in.

  1. On the Android phone, sign into the Google account you will test with.
  2. Open that app's Play link and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install it from Play and open it once.
  4. Stay opted in for 14 days. Do not leave the test. Open the app a few more times during those two weeks.
  5. On iPhone, open the TestFlight link and accept the beta. Apple does not use the 14-day rule.

Only a closed test counts. An internal Play link works only for Gmail addresses already added in Play Console, and those joins do not start the 14 days. Amni-Crypt is the only app on a closed test today. Haven is already on the Play Store, so it does not need this.
