BETA TEST

GOOGLE PLAY & TESTFLIGHT

Google Play holds a new app out of production until twelve testers have stayed opted in for fourteen days straight. The same twelve accounts can cover every app on this page.

HOW TO JOIN

Use the Google account that is on that app’s tester list. Open the Play link, tap Become a tester, install the app, and open it once. Stay opted in for fourteen days. If the count falls under twelve, that app’s fourteen days start over.

On iPhone, open the TestFlight link and accept the beta. TestFlight has no fourteen-day count.

1. THE RIGHT ACCOUNT

Sign in on the phone with the Gmail address added in Play Console for that app.

2. BECOME A TESTER

Open the Play link for the app and accept the test, then install it from Play.

3. FOURTEEN DAYS

Open the app, then leave the test joined. Opting out resets the count for that app.

4. TESTFLIGHT

The iPhone links install through TestFlight. Haven, Amni-Chat, Amni-Learn, and Amni-Type have one.
