THE SHORT VERSION

Amni-CAD is designed to keep technical data on hardware you control. The modeling kernel, the simulation solvers, drawings and quotes all run in your browser, on your computer. The free tier needs no account and has no telemetry or activity logging. Your designs are not uploaded.

A few optional features talk to other services when you turn them on. This page lists each one, what it sends and who receives it. Everything here comes from the Amni-CAD source code and the project documentation. It is a statement of how the software works, not a legal opinion.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

  • Geometry: the OpenCascade modeling kernel runs as WebAssembly in a browser worker.
  • Simulation: stress, modes, heat, flow and mold fill are solved on your own CPU and GPU.
  • Drawings, tolerances and quotes: computed in the page. The quote engine is a WebAssembly module loaded with the app.
  • Saved designs: kept in your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB (your last seven designs and their undo history, so you can recover one) or in files you download. Amni-Scient never receives them.
  • Share links: the whole design is carried inside the link after the #, a part of a web address that browsers do not send to a web server.
  • Shop rates and settings: saved in your browser only.
  • No account, no telemetry: the app has no sign-in for the free tier and contains no analytics, tracking or activity logging. The only network connections the app opens are the optional ones listed below.

Loading the app downloads its own files from amni-scient.com, which is hosted on Cloudflare. That is an ordinary page request, like visiting any website. The app page loads no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics, and it carries a Content-Security-Policy that lists the only outside servers it may contact. The one exception is the optional on-device AI engine, which downloads a runtime script and a model from the hosts named in the table below the first time you use it.

THE ZERO-KNOWLEDGE VAULT (PRO AND ENTERPRISE)

The vault is encrypted part data management inside the editor. Parts, assemblies, BOMs, revisions, check-out locks, change orders and comments are encrypted on each person’s device. The vault server stores ciphertext and a signed, hash-chained log that it can verify but cannot read.

  • Identity: each person has an Ed25519 signing key and an X25519 key, both derived from one 32-byte seed. On disk the seed is sealed with XChaCha20-Poly1305 under an Argon2id key (64 MiB, 3 passes). An admin confirms the key fingerprint out of band when approving a join request.
  • Clearances and compartments: a vault has 1 to 8 clearance levels, each with its own key, and need-to-know compartments, each with its own key. An item is encrypted under all the keys of its level and compartments together. Someone who is not cleared sees only a reserved number and a “Restricted” placeholder.
  • Revocation: lowering a clearance, removing a compartment or removing a member rotates every revoked key and reseals it to the remaining holders only.
  • Files: content-defined chunks are compressed, then sealed with a per-chunk key derived from the item’s classification keys. Chunk IDs are HMACs, so the server cannot confirm guesses about content.
  • Log: every change is signed by its author and chained to the previous entry. Every client replays the log with the same rules, so an entry that breaks a rule is recorded as rejected and changes nothing.

WHAT THE VAULT SERVER CAN SEE

  • Vault IDs, member public keys, roles and clearances
  • The classification labels on each entry and file (not item names or compartment names)
  • Entry counts, sizes and author-supplied timestamps
  • Opaque lock IDs, and chunk counts and sizes
  • Names typed into join requests
  • For the hosted team vault, also the subscription the vault belongs to, how much storage it uses, and the IP addresses that connect, which Cloudflare can see too

The vault server software is separate from the Amni-CAD web app. An Enterprise team can use the vault we host or run Amni-Vault on its own hardware; the encryption is the same either way.

CLASSIFICATION BANNERS ON DRAWINGS (PRO)

Drawings can carry a classification banner and a distribution statement on the sheets. The banner picker offers CUI, CONFIDENTIAL, PROPRIETARY, ITAR CONTROLLED and EAR CONTROLLED, or your own text. Distribution statements A through F are available as written, or you can type your own. When a part is linked to the vault, the banner is filled in from the part’s clearance.

These are markings that you choose and apply. Amni-CAD does not decide what classification or export category your data has.

THE OFFLINE SINGLE-FILE COPY

For machines that are never connected to a network, the Amni-CAD desktop project has a standalone build that packs the whole app, the modeling kernel, the solvers and the quote engine into a single HTML file of about 16 MB. You open it from disk in Chrome or Edge, and it serves its own files from inside the page. Its self-test opens it from disk, builds a part, solves stress and flow, prices and generates a toolpath, makes a drawing and checks that the design is still there after a reload. We ran that self-test on October 15, 2026: all checks passed, and the page made no network requests. A Pro license key is verified on the device, so pasting a key in works offline. Contact us for a copy of the standalone file or for a fixed-term or perpetual license key for an air-gapped site.

EXPORT CONTROL AND COMPLIANCE

Amni-CAD is not certified or accredited for ITAR, EAR or CMMC, and we do not claim that it makes you compliant with any of them. It is designed to keep technical data on hardware you control: computation is local, the free tier sends nothing, optional cloud features are off until you use them, and the vault stores only ciphertext on its server.

Compliance determinations are yours. Whether a given design, tool or workflow meets your obligations, including how you deploy the vault server, which AI features you allow, and who holds license keys, is for your organization and its compliance counsel to decide. If you handle controlled data, you can use the offline copy and leave every optional feature off.
