📐 Measure a space → size any project

Snap or upload a top-down photo (or sketch). Then ① tap two points a known distance apart + enter that distance, ② tap each corner of the area, ③ Use this scan — it saves a real-world outline every footprint app can load.

🧭 How Amni-Scan works (3 tiers)

① WebXR AR tap-to-measure — metric, the real magic

Where the browser supports immersive AR (Android Chrome + headsets today), you point the phone at the floor and tap the corners. ARKit/ARCore returns true real-world distances, so you get a scaled polygon, wall lengths, even roof slope — handed directly to the module's sizing.

② Photo + reference fallback — works everywhere

On iOS Safari (no full immersive-AR yet, as of 2026) or any older device, snap an overhead photo or sketch, set a known reference length, and trace the outline — the same trick the footprint apps already use. Cross-platform, no AR required.

③ Gaussian-splat as-built — the photoreal backdrop

Capture a splat with a phone app (Polycam / Luma / Scaniverse) or an emerging in-browser capture, then load the .ply/.splat here to view your real space in 3D and design the new structure on top of it. Viewer below runs on the PlayCanvas engine.
