AMNI

A 3D collect-a-thon platformer that runs right in your browser. Thirteen realms to explore, 55 canvases to hunt, four transforms to master, and thirteen tyrants to fell — the true masterpiece demands 16 canvases and 4 tyrants. Your progress saves automatically on this device.

Needs WebGPU — works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari 26+ (incl. iOS 26). First load grows the worlds — give it ~15 seconds.
