AMNI

A 3D collect-a-thon platformer that runs right in your browser. Eight worlds to explore, canvases to collect, four transforms to master, and four bosses to beat. Your progress saves automatically on this device.

Best in Edge or Chrome (WebGPU). First load grows the worlds — give it ~15 seconds.
