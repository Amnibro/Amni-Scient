Amni

v2 remake
Classic v1

You are Amni, a robot no bigger than a bolt. Thirteen realms have lost their color. Find the canvases, beat the tyrants that guard them, and paint the world whole.

Runs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox 141+, or Safari 26+ with WebGPU. The first load streams the worlds, so give it a moment. Keyboard and mouse or a gamepad. Your save stays on this device.

  • 13 realms
  • Clock-ring hub
  • 9 skins
  • Own save slots
  • Original score

Controls

WASDMove
SpaceJump, again for double, hold to flutter
ShiftDash, including midair
CtrlCGround-pound; hold to dive in water
FAttack; mash for a 3-hit combo
QThrow cones RCycle
ERead and interact
EscPause; skins live in Options
Saves as savegame_v2_*, classic files stay put. Pause, then the robot skin row, for unlocked plates.
