HumAInity privacy policy

Draft for review. Effective date: November 5, 2026. Publisher: Amni-Scient. Contact: privacy@amni-scient.com.

HumAInity is a strategy game for Android with an optional online war mode. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

What the app itself collects

Nothing, unless you choose to play the online war (below). The single-player game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own. Your saved game, settings, statistics and achievements are stored only on your device. They are removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android's device backup may include them if you have backup turned on.

Online war (optional)

The online war is a shared map where HumAInity and ContAIgion players fight over the same world. It is off until you tap Online war on the title screen; nothing is sent to our server before that, and the live map disconnects when you leave the war screen. The server is run by Amni-Scient at war.amni-scient.com and reached over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection through Cloudflare, our network provider.

Signing in. You can play as a guest or sign in with Google Play Games. Either way we create a random account number and a sign-in token; the token stays in the app and the server keeps only a one-way hash of it. With Play Games, the app gets a one-time code from Google, our server trades it with Google for your Play Games player ID and Play Games name, and then stores only a salted one-way hash of that ID. We never receive your email address, your Google account details or your contacts.

What the server keeps. Your display name (the name you type, or your Play Games name if you leave it blank), your choice about public boards (off unless you turn it on), the outfit you found or join, and your war activity: the orders you give (moves, squads, upgrades, abilities, votes), your funds and your scores. Orders are kept in a season journal so the war can be rebuilt exactly after a restart. If the app stops talking to the server for 3 minutes, you are taken off the map until you return. We also keep side-wide totals for game balance (online counts, ground held, upgrades), with no per-player rows.

What other players see. Your squad and outfit see your display name. Leaderboards and season records list only players who turned on public boards; everyone else plays and scores without appearing there. Outfit names appear on the map only if the founder made them public.

Network data. Our server uses your IP address in memory only, to limit sign-ups and requests from one network; it is never written to disk. Cloudflare processes connection data to carry and protect the traffic.

Deleting it. Forget me on the war screen deletes your account, display name, board entries and sign-in right away. The season journal keeps your past orders under the random account number with no name attached, so the war can still be replayed. You can also email privacy@amni-scient.com.

The online war has no ads of its own and sends nothing to analytics services.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows optional rewarded videos, which you choose to watch for an in-game boost or a revive, and occasionally a full-screen ad between games (never during a game). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To deliver, measure and protect ads, the SDK may collect and share with Google: your device's IP address (which can be used to estimate a general location), your advertising ID or app set ID, app interaction data such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic information about app and ad performance. Data is encrypted in transit. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent choices through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads load. You can change your choices at any time under Settings, Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

HumAInity Premium is an optional one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns Premium, so it can unlock it on your devices.

Other apps

The menu can open our companion game (ContAIgion) or its Google Play page. The app checks only whether that one companion app is installed.

The Codex can show a short link code that summarizes which entries you have filled and whether you own Premium. The code is made on your device and only leaves it if you choose to share it. Codes you enter from the sister version are read on your device and stored there.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell or share personal data. Apart from the online war server, Google Play Games sign-in, and the ads and billing services above, the app does not send data anywhere.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: privacy@amni-scient.com.
