NOTES FROM THE SERVER YOU ALREADY RUN

Point it at your HedgeDoc instance, or demo.hedgedoc.org. The app asks /api/private/config whether you are on 2, then uses REST for 2 and the old Socket.IO save path for 1.x.

1.x AND 2

History, create, edit, pin, delete, revisions. 2 talks /api/private and /api/v2. 1.x still saves over Socket.IO OT.

YOUR LOGIN, YOUR COOKIES

Local username, LDAP, guest, a session cookie, or a HedgeDoc 2 API token. Nothing phones home to Amni-Scient. The session lives on the phone and on your server.

SCIENT THEMES

The same twelve Amni-Scient palettes as Browse: Scient, Emerald, Amni, Haven, Crypt, and the rest, plus Light.
