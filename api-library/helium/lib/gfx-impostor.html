Impostor Views 1

view_dir function

view_dir(i int, j int, n int) float3

Returns a unit view direction for grid cell (i, j) of an n-by-n octahedral-style hemisphere grid.

`view_dir(i int, j int, n int) float3` gives the direction of view (i, j) in an n by n grid. Take u as i over n minus one, times two, minus one, and v the same from j. Let a be half of u plus v and b half of u minus v. The direction is a, then the larger of zero and one minus the absolute values of a and b, then b, normalized with linalg's unit3.

Camera Bases 2

basis function

basis(d float3) float3, float3

Returns the right and up unit vectors of a camera basis perpendicular to the given view direction.

`basis(d float3) (float3, float3)` gives the right and up vectors of a camera looking back along d. Right starts as d's z, zero, and minus d's x; when its length is at most 1e-4 right is the x axis, otherwise right divided by its length. Up is linalg's cross3 of d and right. Return right, then up.
