step function

step(dt real, rate real, dry real, wick real)

Advances the wetness simulation by a time step, applying rain, downhill flow, wicking and drying.

`step(dt real, rate real, dry real, wick real)` advances wetness by `dt` seconds. Take substeps: n is 1 plus the integer part of `dt` times `wick` times 16, h is `dt` over n. Each welded vertex's `mw` is the largest `wet` among its vertices before the first substep.
