pin function

pin(c int, vertex int, x real, y real, z real)

Pins a cloth vertex so it moves to the given world position during steps instead of simulating.

`pin(c int, vertex int, x real, y real, z real)` holds the particle of the cloth's mesh vertex `vertex` at the point: sets its `goal`, and when it was not held yet marks it held, sets its `inv` to 0 and marks the cloth stale. `unpin(c int, vertex int)` frees it again: when held, `held` becomes false, `inv` becomes `inv0` and the cloth is marked stale.

normals function

normals(c int)

Recomputes smoothed per-particle normals for a cloth from its triangles.

`normals(c int)` recomputes the cloth's particle normals: zero them, add each triangle's cross product (the edge from its first to its second corner cross the edge from its first to its third, at the current positions) to its three particles, then scale each to length one, or set (0, 1, 0) where the sum is shorter than 1e-20.
