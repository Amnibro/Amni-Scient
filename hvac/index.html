HVAC duct designer

Draw your system on the Layout tab: drop an air handler, supply registers and returns, then run duct between them. You get live CFM duct sizing, airflow-vs-tonnage, supply/return balance, and a priced materials list.

Double-click the air handler to set tonnage, or a register/return to set its CFM.
Rule of thumb to sanity-check: ~400 CFM per ton of cooling; ~1 register per ~100-150 ft² of room.
