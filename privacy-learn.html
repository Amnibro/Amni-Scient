AMNI-LEARN — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 2026-04-27  |  Last Updated: 2026-04-27

This privacy policy describes how Amni-Learn, a browser-based educational platform developed by Amni-Scient, handles user information.

1. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect no personal data. Amni-Learn has no accounts, no login, no email collection, no analytics, and no Amni-Scient-operated backend. The application runs entirely in your browser. Designed to be safe for children.

2. DATA HANDLING

All games (Pre-K tracing, K-12 math/reading/typing/STEM, adult brain exercise, etc.) execute entirely within your browser. Game state, score, and streak are stored in your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage only — never transmitted off-device. Personal best scores under keys like pb-<game> stay local. Clearing site data removes them.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

3.1 Google AdSense (landing page only)

The Amni-Learn landing page at amni-scient.com/amni-learn.html and the static information sections at the bottom of /learn/ display Google AdSense banners. The interactive games themselves contain no advertising. Google's AdSense SDK may collect cookies and device identifiers per Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to that data.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Amni-Learn is designed for use by children. We do not collect, process, or store any data from any user, regardless of age. There is no behavioral profiling and no personalization that depends on personal information. No COPPA-protected information is processed because no information is processed at all. Parents and educators can use Amni-Learn confidently in classroom and home settings.

5. OFFLINE USE

Once the page has loaded, Amni-Learn works without an internet connection. No game requires a server round-trip.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

Since we collect no personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, export, or delete. To clear local game progress, clear site data for amni-scient.com in your browser settings.

7. CHANGES

Updates will be reflected with a new "Last Updated" date.
