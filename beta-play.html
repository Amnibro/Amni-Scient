Beta program • Android

Join the Android beta

Three steps, about a minute. Google will ask which account to use: each time, pick the one your Play Store uses.

Not sure which one? Open the Play Store app and tap your picture at the top right. The email under your name is the account.

See every beta

  1. Join the testers group

    Pick your Play Store account. Then tap Join group at the top of the page, and Join group again in the box that opens. You only do this once, and it covers every Amni-Scient app.

    Join the testers group

    Done? Come back to this page for step 2.

  2. Become a tester

    Pick the same account, then tap Become a tester.

    Become a tester

  3. Install it

    Tap Install in the Play Store. Updates arrive on their own.

    Open in Google Play

On an iPhone instead? Join on TestFlight.

Stuck? Find what your screen says

Google asks you to sign in, or says the browser “may not be secure”

Sign in with the account your Play Store uses. If Google says the browser or app may not be secure, this page opened inside another app. Tap the ⋮ menu, choose Open in Chrome, and start again.

“You don’t have permission to access this content”

You’re on the group’s page but not in it yet. Tap Join group next to the group’s name at the top, then Join group again in the box.

“App not available” when you try to become a tester

Google doesn’t see that account in the testers group. Do step 1 again and pick the same account in both steps. Then wait a minute and try step 2 again.

“You are a tester”, but the Play Store can’t find the app

The Play Store is using a different account. In the Play Store, tap your picture and switch to the account you picked above. If it’s already the right one, wait 10 minutes and try again: Google can take a little while.

“Your device isn’t compatible with this version”

This test doesn’t support your phone yet, so there’s nothing more to do for now. Thanks for trying!
