Amni-Drone

A QUAD FROM PARTS YOU CAN BUY • WIRING & WEIGHT CHECKED • IN THE BROWSER

OPEN THE STUDIO BUILDS PRIVACY

Amni-Drone is the studio for a small quad you could order and build this week. Every part is a real one, with a maker, a store price and the date it was checked. The studio fits the parts on the frame, checks the wiring and the weight, and draws the model. When it all fits, you export the parts list, the drawing and a frame you can print. Nothing leaves this browser.

REAL PARTS

Motors, props, battery, flight controller, ESC, camera, video and radio. Each card shows the store price and when it was checked. Guessed sizes are marked as guesses.

WIRING & CHECKS

Every wire from pad to pad, with its length. Mounts, prop clearance, weight and power are checked as you swap parts or change the frame size.

EXPORT

A priced parts list, a dimensioned drawing, a frame STL for your printer and the full assembly file. One button trims the printed frame for weight.

Builds

Each build is a set of part files, so a change to one part flows through the frame, the wiring, the drawing and the model.

  • Yard 5 inch. A 5 inch, 4S analog quad on a TBS Source One frame. About 17.5 oz (495.7 g) and $301.39 in parts.
  • Printed yard 5 inch. The same quad on an 8.9 in (226 mm) printed X frame you can make at home.
  • Conceive. Set a prop size, a cell count, the video and a budget, and the studio picks the cheapest parts that fit. Then swap any part by hand.
