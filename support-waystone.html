Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device only, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes it.

How do we play together? Get everyone on the same Wi-Fi, or on one phone's hotspot. On Android, one player taps Play, then Host, and the others see the room in their list or type its address and tap Join. Everyone needs the same version of the game.

Can I host from an iPhone? Not yet. On iPhone you can climb solo and join a friend's room. Hosting is coming.

The game runs hot or slow on my device. Lower the quality, shadows or view distance under Settings, Display.

Do I need an account or an internet connection? No. Solo climbs work offline, and co-op only needs a shared local network.
