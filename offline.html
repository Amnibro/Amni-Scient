OFFLINE

USE AMNI-SCIENT WITH NO CONNECTION

Save an app on this device once. After that it opens and works without the internet.

Checking connection

SAVE AN APP

Each app is downloaded in full and kept in this browser. Small apps are also saved by themselves the first time you open them.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Every page you open while connected is kept, so it opens again with no connection.
  • Saving an app above keeps all of its files, including parts you have not opened yet.
  • Your work in an app (drawings, calculations, progress) is stored on this device and never needed the internet.
  • When you are connected the site always loads the newest version. Saved copies are only used when the network cannot be reached.
  • Saved apps live in this browser on this device. Clearing the browser’s site data removes them. Each browser and device saves its own.

WHAT STILL NEEDS A CONNECTION

  • Anything that talks to another computer: cloud AI assistants, the Amni-CAD remote link, license checks, multiplayer, live weather updates, downloads and sign-in.
  • Amni-Weather shows the most recent forecast pack saved on this device, with its date.
  • Amni-LLM runs models on this device. A model has to be installed while connected; after that it runs offline.

APPS YOU INSTALL

  • Amni-AI (Adam) runs on your own PC. Once a model is installed it answers with no connection.
  • Amni-Chat on Android can pass messages phone to phone over Bluetooth when there is no network, and holds unsent messages until a path opens.
  • Amni-Haven talks to a Haven server you run. A server on your home network keeps working for everyone on that network when the internet is down.
  • Amni-Code works with a model running on your own computer.
