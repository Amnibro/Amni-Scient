AMNI-PRAYER — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 2026-04-27  |  Last Updated: 2026-04-27

This privacy policy describes how Amni-Prayer (Imago Nuntii Divini), a browser-based 3D nodal scripture map developed by Amni-Scient, handles user information.

1. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect no personal data. Amni-Prayer has no accounts, no login, no analytics, and no Amni-Scient-operated server that receives user data. Bible text (35,817 verses) and the cross-reference graph (64,497 cross-references) are embedded in the static page bundle.

2. DATA HANDLING

Reading state, last verse, layout choice, and bookmarks (if any) are stored in your browser's localStorage. Tandem reading mode, pattern analysis, and cross-reference rendering all run client-side via JavaScript and Three.js.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

3.1 Google AdSense (landing page only)

The Amni-Prayer landing page at amni-scient.com/amni-prayer.html displays a Google AdSense banner. The interactive scripture map at /prayer/ shows the Ko-fi support panel rather than ads. AdSense data is collected and processed by Google per Google's Privacy Policy.

3.2 Optional In-Browser AI

Amni-Prayer offers an optional in-browser AI panel for verse exploration via WebLLM (MLC). When enabled, the chosen model (Qwen, SmolLM2, etc.) is downloaded from MLC's CDN and runs entirely in your browser. Prompts and replies do not leave your device.

3.3 Three.js CDN

The 3D nodal renderer loads Three.js from a public CDN (jsDelivr) at first use; CDN access logs may include your IP per the CDN's policy. After first load the asset is browser-cached.

4. CONTENT

Bible text is public-domain Catholic scripture. Cross-references reflect the developer's analytic graph (see the Amni-Prayer terms addendum). Amni-Prayer is not affiliated with any church or denomination.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Amni-Prayer is not directed at children under 13 specifically; it contains no age-restricted content. Because we collect no information, no information from any user is processed.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

Since we collect no personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, export, or delete. To clear local reading state, clear site data for amni-scient.com in your browser settings.

7. CHANGES

Updates will be reflected with a new "Last Updated" date.
