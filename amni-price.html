Amni-Price

SCAN PRICES • COMPARE STORES & RESTAURANTS • FIGHT ALGORITHMIC PRICING

DOWNLOAD APK MORE WAYS TO INSTALL

FREE & OPEN SOURCE (GPL-3.0) • ANDROID 8.0+ • NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS

Stores increasingly set prices with algorithms: by location, by day, sometimes by shopper. Amni-Price is your side of the shelf. Point your phone at a shelf tag or a receipt and it reads the price, matches the product and builds your own record of what things actually cost at every store you visit, so you can see who is really cheapest. Text and barcode reading run on the phone, and your prices never leave it unless you export them.

Screens show sample data.

What it does

  • Live shelf-tag scanning. Walk the aisle and save each price with one tap: price, name, size, barcode and sale flags are read in real time. Multi-buys like “2 for $5” become per-item prices.
  • Receipts. Snap a receipt and every line is read, coupons applied, quantities split into unit prices, and abbreviations like GV WHL MLK matched to products you already logged.
  • Restaurants & delivery apps. Scan menus, menu boards and DoorDash, Uber Eats or Grubhub screenshots. Tag where you saw each price and see the markup, like “Burrito Bowl: DoorDash $13.70, +25% vs $10.95 in store”, plus the same item across a chain’s locations.
  • Compare stores. A cheapest-store ranking, price history charts per store, unit prices, and your personal inflation rate.
  • Price map. Every store’s price for a product on a map, with areas ranked by how expensive they are.
  • Cheapest trip. Your shopping list split between the one or two stores that get it all for the least.
  • Community prices. Optionally download recent prices other shoppers shared with the open Open Prices database for stores near you, anywhere in the world.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account, no ads, no trackers. Your prices, stores and location stay on your phone.

OPEN SOURCE

GPL-3.0 on GitHub. The F-Droid build contains no Google code and is reproducible.

FAST IN THE AISLE

One tap per item while you shop, so logging a whole store run is quick.

Get Amni-Price

  • Direct download: amni-scient.com/downloads/amni-price.apk always gets the newest version (fully open-source build, 36 MB). There’s also a Google ML Kit build (67 MB) with Google’s text recognition.
  • Obtainium (automatic updates from GitHub): add Amni-Price to Obtainium.
  • Google Play (closed beta): join the beta. Not on the tester list yet? Email us with the Google account you use for Play.
  • F-Droid: submitted and passing F-Droid’s build checks; it will appear in the F-Droid app once reviewed.
  • All releases and source: github.com/Amnibro/Amni-Price.

Installing the APK

Tap Download APK on your phone, open the file, and if Android asks, allow your browser to install apps. The direct download, Obtainium and F-Droid builds share one signing key, so you can move between them without losing your data. Google Play installs are signed by Google, so switching to or from Play needs an uninstall first (export your prices to CSV in Settings before you do).
