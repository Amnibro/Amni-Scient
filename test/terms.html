Amni-Test Terms of Service

Draft dated November 4, 2026  |  Not yet in effect

Draft — not yet reviewed by a lawyer. These terms describe how Amni-Test is meant to work. They may change before launch.

Amni-Test is a marketplace on amni-scient.com/test/ where Android app developers pay people to test their apps in Google Play closed testing. It is operated by Amniscient, LLC, a New York limited liability company (“Amniscient”, “we”, “us”). These terms apply to everyone who uses Amni-Test: testers, who test apps, and developers, who post apps for testing. One account can be both. The site-wide Amni-Scient terms also apply; where they conflict with these terms on anything about Amni-Test, these terms win.

1. Who can use Amni-Test

  • You must be at least 18 years old. You confirm this before you take your first test.
  • You sign in with a Google account. You may have only one Amni-Test account, and only one Google account linked to it.
  • You must give accurate information to us and to Stripe, our payment provider.
  • You may not use Amni-Test if we closed an earlier account of yours, unless an appeal reinstated it.

2. What testers do

  • When you accept a test, you agree to opt in to the developer’s closed test with your Google account, install the app from Google Play, keep it installed and use it for 14 days, and file three check-ins (around day 1, day 7 and day 14).
  • Check-ins must be your own honest feedback about the app on your own device. Copied, generated or meaningless feedback is not allowed.
  • You may run up to 3 tests at once.
  • You may not use emulators, device farms, fake devices, or several accounts, or help anyone else do so.
  • You may not test an app you develop or that someone in your household develops.

3. Testers are independent contractors

Testers are independent contractors, not employees, agents or partners of Amniscient or of any developer. You decide whether to accept a test and how you test. You are responsible for your own taxes.

4. Pay for testers

  • You are paid per completed test, not per hour and not per install. A download alone earns nothing.
  • Your price is set by your level when you accept a test, and is capped by the highest level the developer chose. The current levels and prices are listed on the Amni-Test page.
  • A completed test pays your price, less one third for each check-in you missed. A test is completed when the developer confirms it, when the developer does not respond within 72 hours of your day-14 check-in, when the SDK shows the app was used on day 14 or later, or when an admin decides a dispute for you.
  • An excused exit (section 6) pays your price multiplied by the days you tested divided by 14.
  • Earned pay goes to your Amni-Test balance. Payouts are made through Stripe Connect. Stripe verifies your identity and collects any tax forms before your first payout; Stripe’s own terms apply to your Stripe account. You can request a payout once your balance reaches the minimum shown in the app (currently $10).
  • Payouts are paused while your account is under review, banned or terminated.
  • If we pay you by mistake, we may take the amount back from your balance.

5. No payment for ratings or reviews

Amni-Test pays for testing and private feedback only. Nobody is paid to rate, review or comment on an app on Google Play or anywhere else. Developers must not ask testers for ratings or reviews, and testers must not offer them in exchange for anything. Check-ins go to the developer privately. A “helpful” mark means the feedback helped the developer fix or improve the app, not that it was positive.

6. Leaving a test

  • Before you start a test, you may give up the slot at no cost.
  • After you start, you may leave because the app is at fault: it crashes, won’t install, isn’t as described, asks for data it should not, or has offensive content. You must say what happened. The developer may dispute your reason within 72 hours, and an admin decides. If the exit stands, it is excused: you are paid pro rata and get no demerit, and the developer gets a demerit.
  • Leaving for any other reason, or filing no check-ins by the end of day 16, is an unexcused exit: no pay and 5 demerits.

7. Reputation

Each account has a tester standing (merits minus demerits) and a developer standing. The current rules are on the Amni-Test page and in the app. In short, for testers:

  • +3 for a completed test, and +1 when a developer marks a check-in helpful (up to 3 per test).
  • −1 for a missed check-in, −2 for not starting within 72 hours of being added, and −5 for an unexcused exit or an exit reason that loses a dispute.
  • A tester standing of −10 or lower bans the account.

Developers gain standing when testers complete their tests, and lose it for excused exits, lost disputes and upheld reports. A developer standing of −10 or lower blocks new tests. Every change is shown in your activity list with the reason. We may change the reputation rules; changes apply to events after they are published.

8. What developers do

  • You must own the app or be authorized to test it, and the package name you enter must be that app’s.
  • Your app and its listing must follow the law and Google Play’s policies. You may not post malware, apps that collect data without disclosure, or offensive content.
  • You add the testers who accept your test to your Google Play closed test promptly. A tester you don’t add within 72 hours is released.
  • You use testers’ email addresses only to add them to your closed test, and for no other purpose. You may not contact testers outside Amni-Test, add them to mailing lists, or share their addresses.
  • You review completions and exits honestly. Disputes must be made in good faith.
  • If you use the Amni-Test SDK, you use it only in the app you entered and only to confirm installs and use during the test.
  • Amni-Test helps you find testers. It does not promise that Google will approve your app for production or that any number of testers will finish.

9. Developer payments and credit

  • You pay once per test, through Stripe Checkout, for the number of testers and the highest level you choose, plus a platform fee per tester. The price is shown before you pay.
  • Each tester’s actual price is held from your budget when they accept. When a test closes, unused budget, including the fee for slots that didn’t end in a completed test, becomes credit on your account.
  • Credit is applied to your next test automatically. You can ask for unused credit to be refunded to the card you paid with through the contact page. Refunds go back through Stripe.
  • Platform fees for completed tests are not refundable, except where the law requires.
  • Prices are in US dollars.

10. Reports

  • A developer can report a tester who took their test, and a tester can report a developer whose test they took. Categories are harassment, fraud, spam feedback, malware and inappropriate content.
  • When a report is filed, the reported account is placed under review: it can’t take new tests, post apps or receive payouts until the report is resolved. Tests already running continue.
  • An admin reviews each report and upholds or dismisses it. Reports from 2 different users within 30 days terminate the account automatically. We may change that number.
  • False or abusive reports are themselves a breach of these terms.

11. Termination, fraud and the single appeal

  • We may terminate an account that breaks these terms, including after an upheld report. When a tester account is terminated, its running tests end and it is paid for the days already tested, unless the termination is for fraud.
  • Fraud forfeiture. If we find fraud, such as fake devices, several accounts, or fake feedback, the account’s unpaid balance is forfeited and its running tests are not paid.
  • When a developer account is terminated, its open tests close, its testers are excused with pay for the days they tested, and unused budget becomes credit.
  • One appeal. A banned or terminated account may appeal once, ever, from the app. An admin reviews it. If the account is reinstated, a negative tester standing is raised to 0. The decision on the appeal is final.
  • You may stop using Amni-Test at any time. Ask through the contact page to close your account; any balance above the payout minimum can be paid out first.

12. Your content

You keep ownership of your check-ins, app descriptions and other content you submit. You give Amniscient permission to store and show it as needed to run Amni-Test. Testers give the developer of the tested app permission to use their check-ins to improve the app.

13. Disclaimers and limits

Amni-Test is provided “as is”. We don’t control Google Play, Stripe, or the apps being tested, and we aren’t responsible for them. Testers install apps at their own risk. To the extent the law allows, Amniscient’s total liability to you for any claim about Amni-Test is limited to the greater of the amount you paid us or we paid you in the 12 months before the claim, or $100. The disclaimers, limits and dispute terms in the Amni-Scient terms also apply.

14. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of New York, United States, as set out in the Amni-Scient terms. Nothing here removes rights you have under consumer-protection law where you live.

15. Changes

We may update these terms. We’ll post the new version here with a new date and, for significant changes, tell signed-in users in the app. Changes don’t affect tests already accepted.

16. Contact

Questions, refund requests and account requests: use the contact page. Related: Amni-Test privacy policy.
