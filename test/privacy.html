Amni-Test Privacy Policy

Draft dated November 4, 2026  |  Not yet in effect

Draft — not yet reviewed by a lawyer. This policy describes what Amni-Test is built to collect. It may change before launch.

This policy covers Amni-Test, the testing marketplace at amni-scient.com/test/, its server, and the optional Amni-Test Android SDK. Amni-Test is operated by Amniscient, LLC, a New York limited liability company (“we”, “us”). The Amni-Scient privacy policy covers the rest of the site.

1. What we collect

Your account

  • From Google sign-in: your Google email address, the name on your Google account, and Google’s account ID for you. We don’t receive your Google password or your contacts.
  • The date you confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • Your account status, merits, demerits, and a log of every change to your standing or balance with the reason.
  • A session token, stored in your browser, so you stay signed in. We keep only a hash of it.

Testing

  • The tests you take or post, their dates and status, and your claim codes.
  • Check-ins you write: what worked, what broke, and the device you name.
  • Exit reasons and notes, disputes, reports you file or that are filed about you, and an appeal if you make one.
  • For developers: app names, package names, descriptions and instructions you enter.

Payments

  • Stripe handles card payments, identity checks and tax forms. We don’t receive or store card numbers, bank details or ID documents.
  • We store your Stripe account ID, whether payouts are enabled, your balance or credit, and records of payments and payouts.

The Android SDK

If a developer adds the SDK to their app, the app sends to Amni-Test: the app’s package name, a random install ID the SDK creates on first launch, the tester’s claim code (from the Google Play install link, or typed in), and one ping per day when the app is opened. The SDK does not collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, Android ID, IMEI or phone number, and does not collect location, contacts or app usage beyond that daily ping.

What we don’t collect

The Amni-Test pages don’t use analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser stores your session token and a few settings (theme, server address) locally. Our server provider (Cloudflare) processes IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the service.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the marketplace: match testers to tests, track check-ins, compute standing and pay, and settle disputes.
  • To pay testers and charge developers through Stripe.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, such as several accounts or fake installs, and to review reports and appeals.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax and payment records.

We don’t sell personal information and don’t use it for advertising.

3. Who sees what

  • Developers see the Google email address of each tester assigned to their app, because Google Play Console needs that address to add the tester to a closed test. They also see the tester’s Google account name, level, test status, check-ins, exit reasons and notes, and SDK install and ping records for their app. Developers agree to use tester emails only to add them to their closed test.
  • Testers see each open test’s app details and the developer’s standing and completion record. Testers don’t see the developer’s email.
  • Admins (Amniscient staff) see what is needed to review reports, disputes and appeals.
  • Service providers: Stripe (payments and payouts), Google (sign-in) and Cloudflare (hosting and database). Each handles data under its own privacy terms.
  • We may disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect users from fraud or harm.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account, test, check-in and reputation records: while your account is open, and up to 2 years after it closes, so standing, disputes and fraud checks stay accurate.
  • Payment and payout records: as long as tax and accounting law requires (generally 7 years).
  • Session tokens: until you sign out or after 30 days.
  • SDK install IDs and pings: with the test they belong to.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can see your standing history, tests and balance in the app at any time.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account through the contact page. Some records must be kept for the periods above, and deleting an account ends any running tests.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and we’ll honor them.

6. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Session tokens are stored as hashes. Payments and identity checks stay with Stripe. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we’ll tell you as the law requires.

7. Age

Amni-Test is for adults. Nobody under 18 may use it, and we don’t knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes and contact

We’ll post changes here with a new date. Questions or requests: use the contact page. Related: Amni-Test terms of service.
