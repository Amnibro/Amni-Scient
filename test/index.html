Amni-Test • Google Play closed testing

Real testers for your closed test

New personal Google Play developer accounts need 12 testers who stay in a closed test for 14 days before the app can go to production. Amni-Test pays real people to do that properly: install the app, use it, and say what broke.

  • 14 days, 3 check-ins
  • Never asked for Play reviews
  • Paid through Stripe

For testers

You’re paid for finishing a 14-day test and giving honest feedback. A download alone pays nothing. You need an Android phone, a Google account and to be 18 or older.

Pick a test

Open tests show the app, what you’d earn, and how the developer’s past tests went. You can run up to 3 at once.

Join and install

The developer adds your Google email to their closed test. You opt in on Google’s page, then install from Google Play with your own link.

Check in three times

Short forms around day 1, day 7 and day 14: what worked, what broke, which phone. Only the developer sees them.

Get paid

When the test is confirmed, the pay goes to your balance. Cash out to your bank through Stripe once you have $10.00.

Your pay per test depends on your standing: merits minus demerits. Everyone starts at level 1. Your price is fixed when you accept a test.

The rules

Check-ins

  • Day 1: within the first three days.
  • Day 7: during days 7 to 9.
  • Day 14: during days 14 to 17.
  • Each missed check-in costs 1 demerit and a third of the pay.
  • No check-ins at all counts as leaving the test.

Leaving a test

  • If the app is at fault (it crashes, won’t install, isn’t as described, wants data it shouldn’t, or is offensive), say what happened. You’re paid for the days you tested, with no demerit.
  • The developer has 72 hours to dispute that. If they do, an admin decides.
  • Leaving for any other reason costs 5 demerits and pays nothing.
  • Before you start, giving up a slot costs nothing.

Reports, bans and appeals

  • Testers and developers can report each other to an admin.
  • While a report is open, the account can’t take new tests, post apps or cash out. Tests already running continue.
  • Reports from 2 different people within 30 days, or one an admin upholds, close the account. Fraud, like fake devices or several accounts, also loses the balance.
  • A standing of −10 bans the account.
  • Every account gets one appeal. The decision on it is final.

What you’re never asked to do

  • Rate or review an app on Google Play. Nobody is paid for reviews here, and developers may not ask.
  • Install anything from outside Google Play.
  • Share more than your Google email, which the developer needs to add you to the test.

For developers

Google asks new personal developer accounts for at least 12 testers opted in to a closed test for 14 days in a row before applying for production. Amni-Test finds those testers and keeps them engaged.

Post your app

App name, package name, a description and what to test. Choose how many testers and the highest tester level you’ll pay for.

Pay once

One payment through Stripe Checkout covers every slot. Budget you don’t use comes back as credit.

Add testers in Play Console

As testers accept, copy their Google emails or download a CSV. Add them to your closed test’s email list and mark them added.

Read feedback, confirm

Testers check in on days 1, 7 and 14. Mark feedback helpful, then confirm each finished test or dispute it. No answer in 72 hours confirms it.

Optional SDK. A small Android library confirms each install with the tester’s code and sends one ping a day when the app is opened. Tests with a ping on day 14 or later are approved on their own. It sends no device identifiers.

Questions

Why do I sign in with Google?

Developers add testers to a closed test by Google email. Signing in with the same Google account means the email the developer adds is the one your Play Store uses.

What does a developer see about me?

Your Google email and the name on your Google account, your level, and the check-ins you file for their app. They need the email to add you in Play Console. They don’t see your payout details.

How do payouts work?

Payouts go through Stripe. The first time, Stripe asks for your identity and tax details and a bank account. After that, you can cash out whenever your balance is at least $10.00.

What if the developer never adds me?

If they don’t add you within 72 hours of accepting, the slot is released. Nobody gets a demerit.

What if a tester drops out of my test?

While your test is open, the slot opens up again for another tester. The money reserved for the tester who left goes back into your budget.

Can I test my own app?

No. You can test other people’s apps and post your own from the same account, but never both on one app.
